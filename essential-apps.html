Phone setup

Essential apps to download before Japan

A practical setup checklist for the whole family. Install the core apps before leaving, finish offline downloads at home, and only turn on the Japan eSIM after landing at Haneda.

Before leaving

Download Google Maps offline areas, Japanese Translate pack, Navitime, SmartEX, and Ubigi.

At Haneda

Turn on the “Japan Travel” eSIM line, enable data roaming for that line, then test Maps.

Transit ready

Add Suica to Apple Wallet, load about ¥5,000, and enable Express Transit.

Offline trip site

Open this site once on Wi-Fi before departure so the trip pages cache for offline use. Add it to your phone's Home Screen for faster access.

        Must-have survival apps

        SmartEX

        Shinkansen Booked tickets
        • Use for Tokyo to Kyoto and Osaka to Tokyo bullet trains.
        • Keep tickets and reservation numbers accessible before station days.
        • Account is already created for the trip; verify everyone knows where tickets live.

        Google Maps

        Navigation Offline
        • Download offline maps for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka before departure.
        • Save hotels, stations, meeting points, and must-visit shops.
        • Use cached maps if data is weak inside stations or shopping arcades.

        Google Translate

        Menus Offline Japanese
        • Download the Japanese language pack before flying.
        • Use camera mode for menus, signs, ticket machines, and instructions.
        • Keep a few survival phrases saved for taxi, pharmacy, and station help.

        Ubigi eSIM

        Data Activate in Japan
        • The 15-day unlimited Japan data plan is prepaid.
        • Install the eSIM before travel and label it “Japan Travel”.
        • Keep it off until Haneda, then turn on data roaming for that line.

        Japan Travel by Navitime

        Train routing Platforms
        • Use for train platform numbers, transfers, delays, and route timing.
        • Check Shinkansen platform and car details before boarding.
        • Keep as the transit backup if Google Maps is unclear.

        Apple Wallet: Suica

        Transit Tap to pay
        • Add Suica to Apple Wallet before the trip if possible.
        • Load about ¥5,000 with Apple Pay; Mastercard or Amex tends to work best.
        • Enable Express Transit so gates work without Face ID.

        Recommended helpers

        Tabelog, LINE, and ChargeSPOT

        • Tabelog: better local restaurant signal than generic review apps.
        • LINE: useful for restaurant confirmations and Japan-native messaging.
        • ChargeSPOT: rent a phone battery at convenience stores and return it elsewhere.

        Optional safety and convenience apps

        • Yurekuru Call: earthquake and tsunami alerts.
        • XE Currency: yen-to-dollar conversion with offline rates.
        • JNTO app: official tourism info and free Wi-Fi finder.
        • PayPay: optional only if available in your regional App Store.
