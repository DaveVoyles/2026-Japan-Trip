# 🍱 Japan Food Guide (Non-Sushi Focus)

## 📝 Overview

- **Focus:** High-quality non-sushi options for Debbie (Ramen, Tempura, Okonomiyaki, Yakiniku, Bakeries).
- **Budget:** Breakfast/Lunch < $50 USD (7,500 JPY); Dinner < $150 USD (22,500 JPY).
- **Locations:** Aligned with hotel hubs (Ginza, Kyoto Station, Namba).

### 💰 Price Breakdown

- **$** = **Cheap / Budget** (Under 1,500 JPY / ~$10 USD)
- **$$** = **Moderate** (1,500 - 4,000 JPY / ~$10 - $25 USD)
- **$$$** = **Expensive / Upscale** (4,000 - 10,000 JPY / ~$25 - $65 USD)
- **$$$$** = **Luxury / Fine Dining** (Over 10,000 JPY / ~$65+ USD)

## 🏪 Konbini Breakfast Tier List (Grab & Go)

Since we are starting at 6:30 AM, convenience stores are your best friend.

### 🥇 [7-Eleven](https://www.sej.co.jp/in/en.html) (The Gold Standard)

- **Coffee:** "Seven Cafe" (Iced Latte is surprisingly good).
- **Onigiri:** Tuna Mayo (Classic), Salmon (Safe), Soft-Boiled Egg (Rich).
- **Hot Food:** "Nana-Chiki" (Fried Chicken) - yes, for breakfast.
- **Bakery:** Egg Salad Sandwich (Legendary), Sugar Butter Tree cookies.
- 🟢 **Pro:** Best overall food quality and legendary egg sandwiches.
- 🔴 **Con:** Can be the most crowded konbini in major transit hubs.

### 🥈 [Lawson](https://www.lawson.jp/en/) (The Dessert King)

- **Coffee:** "Machi Cafe".
- **Hot Food:** "Kara-age Kun" (Nuggets) - Red is spicy, Regular is savory.
- **Bakery:** **Premium Roll Cake** (Must try).
- 🟢 **Pro:** Superior dessert selection and high-quality "Premium Roll Cake."
- 🔴 **Con:** Hot food variety is slightly less than 7-Eleven or Family Mart.

### 🥉 [Family Mart](https://www.family.co.jp/english.html) (The Fried Chicken King)

- **Hot Food:** **Famichiki** (Boneless fried chicken). Best in class.
- 🟢 **Pro:** Famichiki is the gold standard for convenience store fried chicken.
- 🔴 **Con:** Coffee quality is often considered slightly below 7-Eleven.

## 🍺 Drinking & Nightlife (Enthusiast Level)

### 🥃 Whisky & Cocktails

- **[Bar High Five](https://www.barhighfive.com/) (Ginza):** Legendary cocktails. No menu; tell the bartender your mood.
  - 🟢 **Pro:** World-class bespoke cocktails tailored exactly to your taste.
  - 🔴 **Con:** No menu and high prices can be intimidating for casual drinkers.
- **[Bar Ben Fiddich](https://ameblo.jp/kayama0927/) (Shinjuku):** Farm-to-bar concept. Uses herbs grown by the owner.
  - 🟢 **Pro:** Unique "farm-to-bar" experience with ingredients you won't find anywhere else.
  - 🔴 **Con:** Extremely difficult to get a seat; reservations are a must.
- **[Suntory Whisky House](https://www.suntory.com/whisky/en/house/) (Osaka):** Dedicated to Suntory spirits.
  - 🟢 **Pro:** The best place to sample rare and high-end Japanese whiskies.
  - 🔴 **Con:** Can feel a bit corporate compared to the tiny bars in Shinjuku.

### 🏮 Izakaya & Yokocho (Alleys)

- **[Golden Gai](http://www.goldengai.net/) (Shinjuku):** 200+ tiny bars in 6 alleys. Cover charges apply (~1000 JPY).
  - 🟢 **Pro:** Unmatched atmosphere with hundreds of unique, tiny themed bars.
  - 🔴 **Con:** Many bars have cover charges and some are "members only."
- **[Omoide Yokocho](http://shinjuku-omoide.com/english/) (Shinjuku):** "Piss Alley". Yakitori smoke and cheap beer.
  - 🟢 **Pro:** Gritty, authentic "old Tokyo" vibe with delicious cheap yakitori.
  - 🔴 **Con:** Very narrow, smoky, and can be overwhelming for those who dislike crowds.
- **[Pontocho](https://www.ponto-cho.com/) (Kyoto):** Narrow alley along the river. High-end dining mixed with bars.
  - 🟢 **Pro:** One of the most beautiful and atmospheric dining streets in the world.
  - 🔴 **Con:** Many restaurants are very expensive and require advanced booking.
- **[Hoppy Dori](https://www.asakusa-hoppy-dori.com/) (Asakusa):** Outdoor seating, stewed beef, and "Hoppy" (beer-flavored drink).
  - 🟢 **Pro:** Great for casual, outdoor daytime drinking and local "soul food."
  - 🔴 **Con:** Can get very rowdy and loud, especially on weekends.

## 🛡️ "Safe Haven" Chains (Good, Cheap, Easy)

*Use these when you are tired, hungry, and just want an easy meal with an English menu.*

- **CoCo Ichibanya (Coco Ichi):** 🍛 Japanese Curry. Customizable spice levels (1-10) and toppings (katsu, sausage, veg). Reliable and everywhere.
- **Mos Burger:** 🍔 Japanese burger chain. Try the "Mos Cheeseburger" (meat sauce) or Rice Burger.
- **Sushiro / Kura Sushi:** 🍣 Conveyor belt sushi. Order on a tablet (English available). Cheap and fun.
- **Saizeriya:** 🍝 Italian-style family restaurant. Insanely cheap (pasta for ~$3). The "Milano Doria" is a classic.
- **Tenya:** 🍤 Tempura bowls (Tendon). Fast, cheap, and crispy.
- **Ootoya:** 🍱 Teishoku (set meals). Grilled fish, katsu, healthy options with rice and miso soup.
