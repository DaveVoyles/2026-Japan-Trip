#  Debbie's Shopping Strategy & Wishlist

**Focus:** Stationery, Cosmetics, Lifestyle, Kitchenware, and Japanese Fashion.

##  Stationery & Art Supply (The Holy Grail)

*Japanese stationery is world-class. Dedicate 2-3 hours for these.*

### 1. [Itoya (Ginza)](https://goo.gl/maps/ItoyaGinza)
- **Why:** The "Apple Store" of stationery. 12 floors.
- **Must-Buy:**
  - **Fountain Pens:** Customizable pens on the top floor.
  - **Greeting Cards:** Intricate 3D laser-cut cards (great gifts).
  - **Stickers & Washi Tape:** An entire wall dedicated to them.

### 2. [Loft (Shibuya)](https://goo.gl/maps/LoftShibuya)
- **Why:** Massive variety store. Less upscale than Itoya, but more fun.
- **Must-Buy:**
  - **Planners (Hobonichi Techo):** The gold standard for yearly planning.
  - **Gifts:** Small, cute items perfect for friends back home.

### 3. [Traveler's Factory (Nakameguro)](https://goo.gl/maps/TravelersFactory)
- **Why:** The pilgrimage site for the "Traveler's Notebook".
- **Must-Buy:** Custom leather journal covers and airport-themed refills.

##  Skincare & Cosmetics (J-Beauty)

*Don't buy these at the airport. Buy them at:*

### 1. [@Cosme Tokyo (Harajuku)](https://goo.gl/maps/AtCosmeTokyo)
- **Why:** The #1 ranking store. Every product has a rank based on user reviews.
- **Strategy:** Look for the "No. 1" stickers.
- **Top Picks:**
  - **Melano CC:** Vitamin C serum (fraction of US price).
  - **Hada Labo:** Hyaluronic Acid Lotion (Gold bottle).
  - **Canmake:** Cream Cheek Blush (cult favorite).

### 2. [Don Quijote (Mega Donki - Shibuya)](https://goo.gl/maps/MegaDonkiShibuya)
- **Why:** Chaotic, open 24/7, tax-free.
- **Must-Buy:** Bulk face masks (LuLuLun), strange KitKat flavors, heated eye masks.

### 3. Drugstores (Matsumoto Kiyoshi / Sun Drug)
- **Why:** Cheaper than department stores. Look for the yellow/blue signs everywhere.
- **Must-Buy:** Sunscreen (Biore UV Aqua Rich - buys 5 years worth), Bug Bite Patches (Muhi).

##  Fashion & Lifestyle

### 1. Uniqlo Ginza (Flagship)
- **Why:** 12 floors. Has the "UTme!" custom t-shirt printer.
- **Activity:** Design your own shirt/tote bag with exclusive Japanese stamps/logos in 20 minutes.

### 2. Onitsuka Tiger (Multiple Locations)
- **Why:** Iconic Japanese sneaker brand.
- **Must-Buy:** "Nippon Made" series (Handmade in Japan, incredible leather quality).

### 3. [Starbucks Reserve Roastery (Nakameguro)](https://goo.gl/maps/StarbucksReserveTokyo)
- **Why:** 4 floors of coffee architecture. Merch is exclusive to this location.
- **Warning:** Get a queue ticket FIRST thing in the morning (Use the app or scan QR at door).

---

##  Kitchen & Home (Kappabashi Strategy)

*Kitchen Town (near Asakusa).*

### 1. [Kama-Asa](https://goo.gl/maps/KamaAsa)
- **Why:** Friendly staff, English speaking, high-end knives.
- **Service:** They will engrave your name on the blade for free (takes 10 mins).

### 2. [Kappabashi Street](https://goo.gl/maps/KappabashiStreet)
- **Look For:**
  - **Ceramics:** Sets of 5 small plates (for appetizers).
  - **Fake Food:** Magnets that look like sushi/tempura (classic souvenir).
  - **Chopsticks:** Specialized stores with thousands of designs.

---

##  Tax-Free Reminder

1.  **Bring your Passport:** You must have the physical passport (not a copy) to get 10% off.
2.  **Consumables Bag:** Snacks/Cosmetics will be sealed in a "Do Not Open" bag.
3.  **General Goods:** Clothes/Knives can be used immediately.
