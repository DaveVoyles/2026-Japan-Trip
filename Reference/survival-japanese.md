# Survival Japanese for Travelers

##  The Magic Words
- **Sumimasen** (Soo-mee-mah-sen): Excuse me / Sorry. Use this for EVERYTHING.
- **Arigato Gozaimasu** (Ah-ree-gah-toe Go-zai-mas): Thank you (Polite).
- **Kudasai** (Koo-dah-sai): Please (when ordering).

##  Dining
- **Oishii** (Oh-ee-shee): Delicious.
- **O-kaikei onegaishimasu** (Oh-kai-kay oh-neh-guy-she-mas): Check, please.
- **Eigo no menyu wa arimasu ka?**: Do you have an English menu?

##  Essentials
- **Toire** (Toy-ray): Toilet.
- **Konbini**: Convenience Store.
- **Eki** (Eh-key): Train Station.
