# Survival Japanese for Gamers & Travelers

## 🎮 Retro Gaming Terms

| Japanese | Pronunciation | Meaning | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **箱** | *Hako* | Box | "Hako ari?" (With box?) |
| **説明書** | *Setsumeisho* | Manual | "Setsumeisho ari?" (With manual?) |
| **傷** | *Kizu* | Scratch/Damage | Often written on the price tag. |
| **動作確認済** | *Dousa kakunin zumi* | Tested / Working | Safe to buy. |
| **ジャンク** | *Janku* | Junk | Untested or broken. No refunds. |
| **免税** | *Menzei* | Tax Free | Look for this sign at counters. |
| **見せてください** | *Misete kudasai* | Please show me | Point at a game in the case. |

## 🍜 Dining & Drinking

| Japanese | Pronunciation | Meaning | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **生ビール** | *Nama biiru* | Draft Beer | The standard order. |
| **ハイボール** | *Haibooru* | Highball | Whisky & Soda (very popular). |
| **お会計** | *O-kaikei* | Check/Bill | Say this when leaving. |
| **ごちそうさまでした** | *Gochisousama deshita* | Thank you for the meal | Say to staff when leaving. |
| **おすすめは？** | *Osusume wa?* | Recommendation? | When you don't know what to order. |
| **二人です** | *Futari desu* | Two people | When entering a restaurant. |

## 🏪 Konbini (Convenience Store)

| Japanese | Pronunciation | Meaning | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **温めますか？** | *Atatamemasu ka?* | Warm it up? | Cashier asks for bento boxes. |

## 🗺️ Directions & Help

| Japanese | Pronunciation | Meaning | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ここ** | *Koko* | Here | Point on a map: "Koko made" (to here). |
| **これ** | *Kore* | This | Point to an item you want to buy. |
| **道に迷いました** | *Michi ni mayoimashita* | I'm lost | Use with a map open on your phone. |
| **手伝ってください** | *Tetsudatte kudasai* | Please help me | General help request. |
| **写真を撮ってください** | *Shashin o totte kudasai* | Please take a photo | Hand over phone and ask politely. |

## 🚆 Trains & Navigation

| Japanese | Pronunciation | Meaning | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **次の電車** | *Tsugi no densha* | Next train | Ask staff: "Tsugi no densha wa?" |
| **何番線ですか？** | *Nan-bansen desu ka?* | Which platform? | Critical at big hubs. |
| **各停 / 急行** | *Kakutei / Kyuukou* | Local / Express | Check train type before boarding. |
| **乗り換え** | *Norikae* | Transfer | Signs for connections. |
| **終点** | *Shuuten* | Last stop / Terminus | Avoid overshooting your station. |

## 🏥 Health & Emergencies

| Japanese | Pronunciation | Meaning | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **助けて！** | *Tasukete!* | Help! | Emergency only. |
| **病院** | *Byouin* | Hospital | "Byouin wa doko desu ka?" |
| **薬局** | *Yakkyoku* | Pharmacy | Look for the green cross sign. |
| **頭が痛い** | *Atama ga itai* | I have a headache | Useful at a pharmacy. |
| **アレルギーがあります** | *Arerugii ga arimasu* | I have an allergy | Follow with the item name. |
| **ナッツ / 貝 / 乳製品** | *Nattsu / Kai / Nyuuseihin* | Nuts / Shellfish / Dairy | Pair with the allergy phrase. |

## 🙇 Politeness Boosters

| Japanese | Pronunciation | Meaning | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **お願いします** | *Onegaishimasu* | Please (formal request) | Add after a request to soften it. |
| **大丈夫です** | *Daijoubu desu* | I'm okay / No thanks | Decline politely (e.g., no bag). |
| **すごい！** | *Sugoi!* | Amazing! | Positive reaction. |
| **かわいい！** | *Kawaii!* | Cute! | Shopping or cafes. |

## 🔢 Numbers & Time (Quick)

| Japanese | Pronunciation | Meaning | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **一 / 二 / 三** | *Ichi / Ni / San* | 1 / 2 / 3 | Count people or items. |
| **何時ですか？** | *Nanji desu ka?* | What time is it? | Basic time check. |
| **今から** | *Ima kara* | From now | E.g., "Ima kara arimasu" (available now). |
| **何分？** | *Nan-pun?* | How many minutes? | Ask about wait times. |
