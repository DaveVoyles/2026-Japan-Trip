# 🕹️ Gachapon & Arcade Guide

**For Dave:** The ultimate guide to capsule toys and claw machines.

## 💊 Gachapon (Capsule Toys)

**Cost:** 💴 300 - 500 JPY ($2.00 - $3.50) (Coins 🪙) or IC Card 💳 (Newer machines).
**Etiquette:** Do not leave the empty plastic shells on the floor. Use the designated "Shell Return"
baskets 🗑️ next to the machines.

### 🧠 Strategy

- **Check the Display:** Look for "Sold Out" stickers 🚫.
- **Full Sets:** If you want the *entire* set, go to a "Showcase" store (like in
  [Nakano Broadway](https://nakano-broadway.com/)) and buy the full set. It's cheaper than gambling.

## 👾 Arcades (Game Centers)

**Types of Games:**

1. **UFO Catchers (Claw Machines):** 🛸
   - *Difficulty:* High. They are skill-based, not random.
   - *Tip:* If you spend a lot (~2,000 JPY / $13.50) and fail, ask the staff ("Sumimasen, help?").
     They might reposition the prize for an easy win.
2. **Rhythm Games:** 🥁 (MaiMai, Taiko, Chunithm).
   - Bring gloves 🧤 if you want to look like a pro.
3. **Video Games:** 🕹️ (Gundam, Street Fighter).
   - Usually 💴 100 JPY ($0.65) per credit.

## 📸 Purikura (Photo Booths)

**For Debbie & Dave:**

- **The Gimmick:** Extreme filters ✨ that make your eyes huge and skin porcelain.
- **Process:**
  1. Pay (~💴 500 JPY / $3.50).
  2. Enter Green Screen booth.
  3. Pose fast (countdown is aggressive ⏱️).
  4. Move to "Graffiti Corner" 🖊️ to draw on photos.
  5. Print & Download to phone 📱.
- **Note:** Some "Girls only" areas exist. Couples are usually allowed, but solo men sometimes restricted in Purikura zones.
