#  Gachapon & Arcade Guide

**For Dave:** The ultimate guide to capsule toys and claw machines.

##  Gachapon (Capsule Toys)

**Cost:** 300 - 500 JPY (Coins) or IC Card (Newer machines).
**Etiquette:** Do not leave the empty plastic shells on the floor. Use the designated "Shell Return" baskets next to the machines.

### Best Locations
1. **Gachapon Kaikan (Akihabara):** The OG spot. Cramped, loud, hundreds of machines.
2. **Sunshine City (Ikebukuro):** "Gachapon Department Store". World's largest (3,000+ machines).
3. **Yodobashi Camera (Akihabara/Umeda):** Usually has a massive wall of machines near the entrance.
4. **Narita/Haneda Airport:** Great for using up leftover coins before you fly home.

### Strategy
- **Check the Display:** Look for "Sold Out" stickers.
- **Full Sets:** If you want the *entire* set, go to a "Showcase" store (like in Nakano Broadway) and buy the full set. It's cheaper than gambling.

##  Arcades (Game Centers)

**Types of Games:**
1. **UFO Catchers (Claw Machines):**
   - *Difficulty:* High. They are skill-based, not random.
   - *Tip:* If you spend a lot (~2,000 JPY) and fail, ask the staff ("Sumimasen, help?"). They might reposition the prize for an easy win.
2. **Rhythm Games:** (MaiMai, Taiko, Chunithm).
   - Bring gloves if you want to look like a pro.
3. **Video Games:** (Gundam, Street Fighter).
   - Usually 100 JPY per credit.

### Best Chains
- **Taito Station:** Reliable, good variety.
- **GiGO (formerly SEGA):** Iconic.
- **Round 1:** Often has bowling/karaoke too.

##  Purikura (Photo Booths)

**For Debbie & Dave:**
- **The Gimmick:** Extreme filters that make your eyes huge and skin porcelain.
- **Process:**
  1. Pay (~500 JPY).
  2. Enter Green Screen booth.
  3. Pose fast (countdown is aggressive).
  4. Move to "Graffiti Corner" to draw on photos.
  5. Print & Download to phone.
- **Note:** Some "Girls only" areas exist. Couples are usually allowed, but solo men sometimes restricted in Purikura zones.
