# 🛍️ Shopping Wishlist & Strategy

**Focus:** Retro Video Games, Japanese Exclusives, Unique Souvenirs.

## 🎮 Retro Video Games (The Holy Grail)

**Strategy:** Prices in Akihabara are high (tourist tax). Dig deeper in Nakano, Osaka, or "Hard Off" stores in the suburbs if time permits.

### 📍 Tokyo: Akihabara (The Electric Town)

- **[Super Potato](https://www.superpotato.com/):** The museum-like flagship. Expensive, but amazing selection.
  Go for the vibes and photo ops. Top floor has a retro arcade.
- **[Mandarake Complex](https://www.mandarake.co.jp/dir/cmp/index-en.html):** 8 floors of otaku culture.
  Great for boxed games and rare items.
- **[Surugaya](https://www.suruga-ya.jp/):** Often has better prices than Super Potato. Chaotic shelves, good digging.
- **[Trader](http://www.e-trader.jp/):** Good for newer retro (PS2, GameCube, DS).
- **[Book Off](https://www.bookoff.co.jp/shop/shop20424.html) (Akihabara):** Check the basement.

### 📍 Tokyo: Nakano Broadway

- **[Mandarake Galaxy](https://www.mandarake.co.jp/dir/gxy/index-en.html):** Dedicated to video games.
- **Rare & Vintage:** Lots of small stalls selling obscure toys and games.

### 📍 Osaka: Den Den Town (Nipponbashi)

- **[Super Potato](https://www.superpotato.com/shop/osaka_nipponbashi/) (Osaka Branch):** Often less crowded than Tokyo.
- **[Game Tanteidan](https://www.tanteidan.cc/):** A hidden gem for serious collectors.
- **[Joshin Super Kids Land](https://shop.joshin.co.jp/shopdetail.php?cd=1746):** New kits and games.

### 🛒 What to Look For (Japanese Exclusives)

- **Nintendo:** Famicom/Super Famicom carts (beautiful art).
- **Sega:** Saturn and Dreamcast games (region locked, but great for shelves).
- **Handhelds:** Game Boy / GBA / Vita (often region-free).
- **Condition Codes:**
  - **A:** Mint / New.
  - **B:** Good / Used.
  - **C:** Damaged / Missing manual (Huge discount).
  - **Junk:** Broken/Untested (Risky, but cheap).

## 🎎 Souvenirs & Other Goods

### 🥢 Kitchen & Home

- **[Kappabashi](https://www.kappabashi.or.jp/en/) (Tokyo):** "Kitchen Town".
  - **Knives:** High-quality Japanese steel (bring passport for tax-free).
  - **Plastic Food Samples:** The classic fake sushi/ramen displays.
  - **Ceramics:** Bowls and sake sets.

### 🍬 Snacks & Consumables

- **[Don Quijote (Donki)](https://www.donki.com/en/):** The chaotic discount store.
  Buy KitKats (Sake, Matcha, Wasabi flavors) here in bulk.
- **Pharmacy (Drug Stores):** Rohto Lycee eye drops, Biore sunscreen, skincare.

### 👕 Fashion

- **Uniqlo / GU:** Cheaper than in the US.
- **Shimokitazawa:** Vintage clothing (thrift store heaven).
