# Shopping Wishlist

##  Debbie's Shopping
### Stationery & Cute Items
- [ ] **Itoya (Ginza):** 12 floors of stationery heaven.
- [ ] **Loft (Shibuya/Ginza):** Massive variety of goods.
- [ ] **Tokyu Hands:** DIY and creative goods.
- [ ] **Kyukyodo (Ginza):** Traditional Japanese paper and incense.

### Cosmetics
- [ ] **Matsumoto Kiyoshi:** The ubiquitous drugstore.
- [ ] **Cosme Kitchen:** Organic/Natural cosmetics.

##  Dave's Retro Gaming
### Akihabara
- [ ] **Super Potato:** The famous retro tower.
- [ ] **Mandarake Complex:** 8 floors of otaku goods.
- [ ] **BEEP:** Vintage PC and obscure hardware.

### Osaka (Den Den Town)
- [ ] **Super Potato (Osaka Branch)**
- [ ] **Surugaya:** Often cheaper than Akihabara.
