#  Cultural Etiquette & Manners

**Golden Rule:** Be quiet , be clean , be aware of your surroundings .

##  Trains & Transit
- **Silence:**  No phone calls. Keep voice down.
- **Backpacks:**  Wear them on your front or put them on the rack if the train is crowded.
- **Eating:**  Only acceptable on Shinkansen (Green Car/Reserved) or long-distance trains. Never on subways.
- **Priority Seats:**  Do not sit here if you are able-bodied, or move immediately if an elderly/pregnant person enters.

##  Dining
- **Chopsticks:**
  - Never stick them vertically in rice (funeral ritual) .
  - Never pass food chopstick-to-chopstick (funeral ritual).
  - Use the chopstick rest when not eating.
- **Slurping:**  Slurping noodles (Ramen/Soba) is polite and shows enjoyment.
- **Tipping:**  **DO NOT TIP.** It is confusing and rude. The price is the price.
- **Oshibori:**  Use the wet towel to clean your hands before eating, not your face.

##  Trash
- **No Public Bins:** You will carry your trash all day. Bring a plastic bag .
- **Vending Machines:**  Usually have a recycling bin next to them for bottles/cans ONLY.
- **Konbini:**  Convenience stores have bins for customers.

##  Walking
- **No Eating & Walking:**  It is considered sloppy. Eat at the shop or find a bench.
- **Escalators:**
  - **Tokyo:** Stand Left, Walk Right .
  - **Osaka:** Stand Right, Walk Left .
- **Smoking:**  Only in designated smoking areas. Street smoking is often fined.

##  Temples & Shrines
- **Torii Gates:**  Bow once before entering and leaving.
- **Purification:**  Wash hands/mouth at the water pavilion (Chozuya) before approaching the main hall.
- **Photography:**  Respect "No Photo" signs, especially of Buddha statues.

##  Onsen / Public Baths
- **Wash First:**  You must shower thoroughly *before* entering the bath water.
- **No Swimsuits:**  You must be naked.
- **Tattoos:**  Often banned. Check ahead or use "tattoo-friendly" stickers if small.
- **Hair:**  Tie long hair up so it doesn't touch the water.
