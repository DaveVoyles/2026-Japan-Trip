#  Power User Google Maps & Pin Guide

**WHY:** "Shinjuku Station" is a city, not a point. You will get lost.
**HOW:** Click these links to save exact locations to your "Favorites" list in Google Maps.

##  Hotel Entrances (The "Where is the Door?" Problem)

*   **[Keio Plaza Hotel (Shinjuku) Main Entrance](https://goo.gl/maps/KeioPlazaShinjuku)**
    *   *Note:* The lobby is on the 3rd floor if arriving by foot from the station. The 1st floor is for buses/taxis.
    *   *Limousine Bus Stop:* Right outside the 3rd-floor lobby main doors.

*   **[Cross Hotel Kyoto (Kawaramachi)](https://goo.gl/maps/CrossHotelKyoto)**
    *   *Note:* Entrance is tucked away slightly from the main street. Look for the "Cross" logo pillar.

*   **[Cross Hotel Osaka (Dotonbori)](https://goo.gl/maps/CrossHotelOsaka)**
    *   *Note:* The lobby is NOT on the ground floor. Take the elevator up. The entrance is near the giant "Running Man" sign.

##  Station Meeting Spots (When You Are Lost)

*   **[Shinjuku Station: East Exit Police Box (Koban)](https://goo.gl/maps/ShinjukuEastKoban)**
    *   *The universal meeting spot.* If you get split up in Shinjuku, meet here. It's next to the Studio Alta screen.

*   **[Tokyo Station: Marunouchi Central Gate](https://goo.gl/maps/TokyoStnMarunouchi)**
    *   *The Grand Facade.* The beautiful red brick side.
    *   *Gransta Tokyo (Food):* B1 Floor inside the gates.

*   **[Kyoto Station: The "Big Stairs" (Grand Staircase)](https://goo.gl/maps/KyotoStationStairs)**
    *   *Landmark:* 171 steps leading to the sky garden. Impossible to miss.

##  Retro Gaming Hard-To-Find Entrances

*   **[Super Potato Akihabara (Entrance)](https://goo.gl/maps/SuperPotatoAkiba)**
    *   *Trick:* It's a tiny elevator lobby. Look for the Pac-Man ghosts on the narrow building facade.
    *   *Floor:* 3F, 4F, 5F.

*   **[Mandarake Galaxy (Nakano Broadway)](https://goo.gl/maps/MandarakeGalaxy)**
    *   *Trick:* 2nd Floor of Nakano Broadway mall. Look for the wall of CRT TVs displaying static.

*   **[Game Tanteidan (Osaka)](https://goo.gl/maps/GameTanteidan)**
    *   *Trick:* Easy to miss. Look for the yellow sign with brown text.

##  Shopping & Logistics

*   **[Yamato Transport (Kyoto Station Counter)](https://goo.gl/maps/YamatoKyoto)**
    *   *For:* Same-day luggage forwarding to hotels.
    *   *Location:* B1 Floor (Porta underground mall) or Hachijo Exit (check specific counter).

*   **[Don Quijote (Dotonbori - Ferris Wheel)](https://goo.gl/maps/DonkiDotonbori)**
    *   *Note:* The entrance is chaotic. Go to the *top floor* first and work your way down to avoid crowds.

*   **[Kappabashi "Kitchen Town" Entrance (start here)](https://goo.gl/maps/KappabashiStart)**
    *   *Why:* The street is long. Start at the "Niimi" building (giant chef head on the roof).

##  Emergency Locations

*   **[Tokyo Metropolitan Police Dept (Lost & Found)](https://goo.gl/maps/TokyoLostFound)**
    *   *飯田橋 (Iidabashi).* Where lost passports/wallets end up.

*   **[St. Luke's International Hospital (Tokyo)](https://goo.gl/maps/StLukesHospital)**
    *   *English Speaking ER.*
