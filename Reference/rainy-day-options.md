#  Rainy Season Contingency Guide (Tsuyu)

**Context:** Late May to Early June is the start of the rainy season.
**Rule:** If it rains < 5mm, use a convenience store umbrella. If it POURS (>10mm), change plans.

##  Tokyo: The Indoor Metropolis

*Use the extensive underground network to stay dry.*

### 1. The "Otaku" Bunker (Nakano Broadway)
- **Why:** Entirely indoors. You can spend 4-6 hours here easily.
- **Plan:** Lunch in the basement, shop levels 2-4 (Mandarake), coffee on level 2.
- **Get There:** Tozai Subway Line to Nakano Station -> Covered Arcade (Sun Mall) -> Broadway. **100% Covered walk.**

### 2. Joypolis (Odaiba)
- **Why:** Massive indoor SEGA theme park. Rides, games, VR.
- **Note:** Decks Tokyo Beach mall is attached. Legoland Discovery Center is also here.
- **Get There:** Yurikamome Line to Odaiba-Kaihinkoen.

### 3. TeamLab Planets (Toyosu)
- **Why:** Entirely indoors (barefoot experience).
- **Note:** You already have this on the list, but it's a perfect rain activity.

### 4. Sunshine City (Ikebukuro)
- **Why:** "City within a City". Aquarium (top floor), Pokemon Center, Gachapon Department Store, massive shopping mall.
- **Get There:** Underground passage from Ikebukuro Station (Exit 35).

##  Kyoto: The "Dry" Temple Route

*Don't hike Fushimi Inari in a downpour. Do this instead.*

### 1. Sanjusangen-do (Hall of 1001 Kannons)
- **Why:** Massive indoor wooden hall. The main attraction is entirely under a roof.
- **Bonus:** It's right across from the Kyoto National Museum (also indoors).

### 2. Nishiki Market & Teramachi Arcade
- **Why:** Kilometers of covered shopping streets.
- **Route:** Start at Nishiki Market -> walk to Teramachi -> walk to Shinkyogoku. You can walk from Karasuma to Gion almost entirely under cover.

### 3. Kyoto Railway Museum
- **Why:** Huge, modern, and mostly indoors. Great for tech/train history.
- **Get There:** Bus or short walk from Umekoji-Kyotonishi Station.

##  Osaka: The Underground City

*Osaka has the best underground mall network in Japan.*

### 1. Umeda Underground Labyrinth
- **Why:** Whity Umeda, Diamor Osaka, and Hankyu Sanbangai connect 7 stations and 1000+ shops underground.
- **Activity:** Shop at Hep Five (Ferris wheel on top), eat at the underground food streets.

### 2. Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan
- **Why:** One of the world's largest. 95% indoors.
- **Bonus:** The Tempozan Marketplace next door offers food and Legoland.

### 3. Spa World (Shinsekai)
- **Why:** Massive indoor onsen theme park. "European Zone" vs "Asian Zone".
- **Rules:** No tattoos allowed (usually strict). If you have tattoos, skip this.

##  Emergency Gear

- **Transparent Umbrella (Vinyl):** 500-700 at any Seven-Eleven. Don't pack one; buy it there.
- **Waterproof Shoes:** Use waterproof spray on your walking shoes BEFORE you leave the hotel.
- **The "Department Store" Hack:** If caught in rain, duck into any Depato (Isetan, Takashimaya). They will put a plastic cover on your shopping bags for you at the entrance.
