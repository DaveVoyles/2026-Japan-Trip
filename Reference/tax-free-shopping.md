# 🛍️ Tax-Free Shopping Guide

**The Benefit:** Save 10% consumption tax on purchases over 5,500 JPY (~$37 USD).
**Estimated Savings on This Trip:** ¥5,000-15,000 ($33-100) if you're buying electronics, clothing, or souvenirs!

---

## ✅ Requirements

1. **Passport:** 🛂 You MUST have your physical passport with you. Photos/copies do not count.
2. **Tourist Status:** ✈️ You must be on a "Temporary Visitor" entry stamp (standard for tourists from US).
3. **Minimum Spend:** 💴 **5,500 JPY** (excluding tax) at a _single shop_ on a _single day_.
   - **Note:** Some stores have separate minimums: ¥5,500 for consumables AND ¥5,500 for general goods
4. **Within 6 Months:** Must take items out of Japan within 6 months of purchase (you'll be gone in 9 days, so no problem!)

---

## 🔄 The Process (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Look for the Sign 🌸

Look for the **"Japan Tax-Free Shop"** logo (cherry blossom with "Tax-Free" text) at store entrance or register.

**Major Chains (Always Tax-Free):**

- 🐧 **[Don Quijote (Donki)](https://www.donki.com/en/)** - Discount variety store, HUGE tax-free selection
- 📷 **[Bic Camera](https://www.biccamera.com/bc/main/)** - Electronics, appliances, toys
- 📱 **[Yodobashi Camera](https://www.yodobashi.com/)** - Electronics superstore
- 👔 **[Uniqlo](https://www.uniqlo.com/jp/en/)** - Clothing (select flagship stores only)
- 💄 **[Matsumoto Kiyoshi](https://www.matsukiyo.co.jp/english)** - Drugstore (cosmetics, medicine)
- 🏬 **Department Stores:** Isetan, Mitsukoshi, Takashimaya, Matsuya

**Small Shops:**

- Many retro game shops, anime stores, souvenir shops do NOT offer tax-free
- Always ask "Tax free desu ka?" (Is this tax free?) before buying

**Where You'll Shop Most:**

- **Akihabara:** Super Potato, Mandarake, Surugaya (some branches tax-free, ask first!)
- **Den Den Town:** Similar to Akiba, check each store
- **Shibuya Parco:** Nintendo Store, Pokemon Center (tax-free available)

### 3. "The Bag" Rule (Important!) 🛍️

**Consumables (Snacks, Cosmetics, Medicine):**

- **Rule:** Must be sealed in a special clear "Tax-Free" bag at the register.
- **Restriction:** **DO NOT OPEN** this bag while in Japan.
- **Why:** If customs sees it open, they can charge you the 10% tax.
- **Packing Tip:** These bags are bulky and full of air. It makes tetris-packing your suitcase hard.
  - _Strategy:_ Don't buy tax-free snacks unless you are buying A LOT. The small savings (¥500 on ¥5000) isn't worth the packing headache.

**General Goods (Games, Clothes, Shoes, Electronics):**

- **Rule:** Usually **NOT** sealed in a bag.
- **Benefit:** You **CAN** use/wear them in Japan immediately!
- **Dave's Retro Games:** You _can_ open your Super Potato finds, test them, and pack them normally in your suitcase. NO sealed bag required!

### 4. At the Airport (Departure) 🛫

- **Scan Receipt:** Scan your passport at the automated gate after security/immigration.
- **Show Goods:** Extremely rare. Customs officers almost never ask to see the physical items unless you bought luxury watches/bags ($10k+).
- **Liquids:** If you bought tax-free sake/cosmetics >100ml, put them in your **CHECKED** bag. Customs knows this contradiction exists; it's allowed.

| Category          | Minimum | Examples                                    | Sealed?             |
| :---------------- | :------ | :------------------------------------------ | :------------------ |
| **Consumables**   | ¥5,500  | Food, drinks, cosmetics, medicine, vitamins | ✅ YES (sealed bag) |
| **General Goods** | ¥5,500  | Electronics, clothing, games, toys, books   | ❌ NO seal          |

**Pro Tip:** At stores like Don Quijote, buy both consumables and general goods to easily hit both minimums!

### 3. At the Register 💳

**Step-by-Step:**

1. **Finish Shopping:** Bring items to register
2. **Say:** "Tax-free onegaishimasu" (Tax-free please)
3. **Present Passport:** Hand your physical passport to cashier (they need to scan entry stamp)
4. **Wait:** Cashier processes tax-free form (takes 2-5 minutes)
5. **Scan:** They scan your passport QR code and record purchase
6. **Seal Consumables:** If you bought food/cosmetics, they put them in sealed bag (DON'T OPEN!)
7. **Sign Form:** Sign the tax-free form (they attach it to your passport)
8. **Pay:** Pay the **pre-tax price** (10% discount applied!)
9. **Keep Receipt:** Save the tax-free receipt for customs at airport

**What They'll Staple to Your Passport:**

- A special tax-free form showing your purchases
- This gets removed at the airport when you leave Japan

**Payment Methods:**

- Most places accept Visa/Mastercard for tax-free purchases
- Some small shops require cash (bring yen!)

### 4. Consumables vs. General Goods (Important!)

#### 🍬 Consumables (Food, Cosmetics, Medicine)

**Examples:**

- Snacks (Kit Kats, Pocky, instant ramen)
- Cosmetics (SK-II, Shiseido, Japanese skincare)
- Medicine (pain relievers, cold medicine)
- Vitamins and supplements
- Toiletries (shampoo, body wash)
- Alcohol and beverages

**The Sealed Bag Rule:** 🛍️

- Shop puts items in **special clear plastic bag with red border**
- Bag is **sealed** with tamper-evident sticker
- **DO NOT OPEN** until you leave Japan (after customs at airport!)
- If opened in Japan, you technically owe the tax (though rarely enforced)
- **Packing:** Put sealed bags in checked luggage OR carry-on (liquids >100ml must be checked)

**What If I Want to Use Cosmetics?**

- Buy non-tax-free at the register, OR
- Make separate purchases (one tax-free sealed, one regular to use now)

#### 👕 General Goods (Electronics, Clothing, Games)

**Examples:**

- Video games and game consoles
- Electronics (cameras, headphones, portable chargers)
- Clothing and shoes
- Toys and figures
- Books and manga
- Bags and accessories

**No Seal Required:** ✅

- You CAN use these immediately!
- Wear the clothes, play the games, use the electronics
- Just must take them out of Japan within 6 months (no problem for you!)

### 5. Multiple Stores Strategy 🏬

**Department Stores (Isetan, Mitsukoshi, Takashimaya):**

- Shop at MULTIPLE boutiques inside the department store
- Bring all receipts to **Tax Refund Counter** (usually on 1st or top floor)
- They combine receipts to hit ¥5,500 minimum
- **Note:** Must be same day, same department store building

**Shopping Malls (Shibuya Parco, etc.):**

- Some malls have central tax-free counter
- Others require tax-free per individual shop (check at info desk)

**Stand-Alone Shops:**

- Each shop is separate - can't combine receipts between different stores
- Plan accordingly!

## 🛫 At the Airport (Departure) - IMPORTANT!

### When Leaving Japan:

**Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND) - Tokyo:**

1. **Check In:** Normal flight check-in first
2. **Customs Declaration:** Before security, there's a "Customs" area
   - Look for signs saying "Tax-Free" or "Customs Inspection"
   - If you have nothing to declare, you can often skip the line
3. **Automated Gates:** Most airports now have automated passport gates that scan tax-free forms
   - Scan passport at machine
   - It removes the tax-free forms electronically
   - Keep walking!
4. **Random Inspection:** Rare, but customs MAY ask to see purchases
   - Have sealed bags accessible in carry-on (easier to show)
   - If in checked luggage, they can't easily inspect (usually wave you through)

**Kansai (KIX) - Osaka:**

- Similar process
- Automated gates available
- Customs area is before immigration

**What Happens to Forms:**

- Airport customs removes the forms stapled to your passport (or removes electronically)
- You keep your receipts as souvenirs

**Do They Actually Check?**

- **99% of the time:** No, it's automated and quick
- **1% random inspection:** They might ask to see sealed bags
- **Tip:** Keep sealed consumables bag accessible in carry-on for quick inspection if asked

## 💡 Pro Tips & Strategies

### Maximize Savings:

1. **Plan Big Purchases:** If buying expensive electronics or multiple games, use tax-free!
2. **Combine Shopping:** Shop at same store on same day to hit minimum
3. **Don Quijote Strategy:** Buy snacks + cosmetics + souvenirs all at once → Easy ¥10,000+ purchase
4. **Time Your Purchases:** Shop tax-free mid-trip, not Day 1 (so you have bags to pack items)

### Timing Tips:

- **Don Quijote:** Go late night (10 PM-midnight) or early morning (9-10 AM) to avoid tourist lines
- **Bic Camera:** Weekday mornings less crowded
- **Shibuya Parco:** Right when it opens (10 AM) to avoid Nintendo Store crowds

### Common Mistakes to Avoid:

- ❌ Forgetting passport (can't get tax-free without it!)
- ❌ Opening sealed consumables bag before leaving Japan
- ❌ Buying from multiple stores thinking you can combine receipts later
- ❌ Trying to use photocopy of passport (must be physical passport)
- ❌ Shopping at tiny shops that don't offer tax-free (ask first!)

### Best Stores for Your Trip:

**For Dave (Gaming/Electronics):**

- ⚠️ **Mandarake:** SOME branches tax-free, ASK before purchasing
- ⚠️ **Surugaya:** Hit or miss, check each location

**For Debbie (Cosmetics/Shopping):**

- ✅ **Matsumoto Kiyoshi:** Best for cosmetics tax-free, everywhere
- ✅ **Don Quijote:** Great for snacks, cosmetics, quirky souvenirs
- ✅ **Department Store Beauty Counters:** Isetan, Mitsukoshi (Ginza)

**For Both (Souvenirs):**

## 📋 Tax-Free Shopping Checklist

**Before Shopping:**

- [ ] Carry physical passport (leave in hotel safe when not shopping)
- [ ] Know which stores offer tax-free (check entrance for logo)
- [ ] Plan to spend ¥5,500+ at same store on same day

**At the Store:**

- [ ] Say "Tax-free onegaishimasu" at register
- [ ] Present passport
- [ ] Sign tax-free form
- [ ] Receive sealed bag for consumables (DON'T OPEN!)
- [ ] Keep receipt with sealed bag

**Packing for Flight:**

- [ ] Sealed consumables in checked luggage OR carry-on
- [ ] General goods packed normally
- [ ] Keep one sealed bag accessible for potential customs check
- [ ] Don't open sealed bags until after customs at airport!

**At Airport Departure:**

- [ ] Go through customs area (before or after security depending on airport)
- [ ] Scan passport at automated gate
- [ ] Forms get removed from passport
- [ ] If asked, show sealed bags quickly
- [ ] After customs, you can open sealed bags!

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

| Problem                       | Solution                                                                      |
| :---------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Forgot passport**           | Can't get tax-free. Pay full price OR return later with passport              |
| **Under ¥5,500**              | Buy more items to hit minimum, OR pay full price                              |
| **Store says "not eligible"** | You might not have tourist stamp in passport (need "Temporary Visitor" stamp) |
| **Opened sealed bag early**   | Technically owe tax, but rarely enforced. Don't worry too much.               |
| **Lost tax-free receipt**     | Keep the sealed bag at least. Customs rarely asks for receipt.                |
| **Sealed bag broke**          | Items are fine to take home, just reseal if possible                          |
| **Customs asks to see items** | Show the sealed bags calmly, they'll wave you through                         |
