#  Tax-Free Shopping Guide

**The Benefit:** Save 10% consumption tax on purchases over 5,500 JPY (~ USD).

##  Requirements
1. **Passport:** You MUST have your physical passport with you. Photos do not count.
2. **Tourist Status:** You must be on a "Temporary Visitor" entry stamp (standard for tourists).
3. **Minimum Spend:** 5,500 JPY (excluding tax) at a *single shop* on a *single day*.

##  The Process

### 1. Look for the Sign
Look for the "Japan Tax-Free Shop" logo (usually a cherry blossom symbol) at the register.
- *Major Stores:* Don Quijote, Uniqlo, Bic Camera, Yodobashi Camera, Department Stores.
- *Small Shops:* Many do not offer it. Ask "Tax Free?"

### 2. At the Register
- **Present Passport:** Hand it to the cashier before paying.
- **Scan:** They will scan your entry stamp/QR code.
- **Pay:** You pay the pre-tax price immediately (or get a refund at a service counter in department stores).

### 3. Consumables vs. General Goods

####  Consumables (Snacks, Cosmetics, Liquids)
- **Sealed Bag:** The shop will seal these in a special clear plastic bag.
- **Rule:** **DO NOT OPEN** this bag until you leave Japan. If you consume them in Japan, you owe the tax.

####  General Goods (Clothes, Electronics, Games)
- **No Seal:** You can use these immediately (e.g., wear the shoes, play the game).
- **Condition:** Must leave Japan with you within 6 months.

##  At the Airport (Departure)
- **Customs:** After security but before immigration, there is a "Customs" desk.
- **Scan:** Scan your passport. They digitally verify your tax-free purchases.
- **Inspection:** Rarely happens, but they *can* ask to see the items. Keep them in your carry-on or easily accessible in checked luggage if possible (though liquids must be checked).

##  Tips
- **Combine:** In large malls (like Aeon or Parco), you can sometimes combine receipts from multiple shops to hit the 5,500 JPY minimum. Go to the "Tax Refund Counter".
- **Don Quijote:** The tax-free line is often separate and can be very long. Go late at night or early morning.
