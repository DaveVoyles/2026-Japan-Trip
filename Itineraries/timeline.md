# Trip Timeline & Checklist (October 2026)

## 🏗️ Phase 1: The Foundation (Now - Jan 2026)

### 10-11 Months Out

- [ ] **Book Flights (CRITICAL)**
  - **Window:** Open now (330 days in advance).
  - **Action:** Monitor prices for EWR -> HND and KIX -> EWR.
  - **Target:** Premium Economy (~$4,400 - $7,000 total).
  - *Note:* October is peak season. Book sooner rather than later.

## 🏨 Phase 2: Accommodation (Jan - April 2026)

### 6-9 Months Out

- [ ] **Book Hotels**
  - **Tokyo:** 4 Nights (Ginza or Shinjuku).
  - **Kyoto:** 3 Nights (Near Kyoto Station or Gion).
  - **Osaka:** 1 Night (Near Namba or Osaka Station).
  - *Strategy:* Book refundable rates now to lock in price/availability. You can cancel later if you find a better deal.

## 🎫 Phase 3: The "Hard" Tickets (August 2026)

### 2 Months Out

- [ ] **[USJ Express Pass](https://www.usj.co.jp/web/en/us/tickets)** (CRITICAL)
  - **Window:** Tickets release exactly **2 months** before the date.
  - **Action:** If visiting Oct 16, be on the website Aug 16 at midnight JST (which is morning EST).
  - **Why:** Required for Super Nintendo World & Donkey Kong Country entry without 3-hour lines.
- [ ] **[Ghibli Museum](https://www.ghibli-museum.jp/en/tickets/)** (CRITICAL)
  - **Window:** Tickets release on the 10th of the month at 10:00 AM JST, 2 months prior.
  - **Action:** If visiting Oct 17 (Day 4), be ready on Aug 10 at 10:00 AM JST.
  - **Booking:** Via [Lawson Tickets](https://l-tike.com/en/) online or at Loppi kiosks in Japan.
  - **Why:** Tickets sell out within minutes. Have backup plan ready.

## 🍽️ Phase 4: Dining & Specifics (September 2026)

### 1 Month Out

- [ ] **[Pokemon Cafe](https://www.pokemoncenter-online.com/cafe/en/reservation.html) / [Kirby Cafe](https://kirbycafe.jp/en/)**
  - **Window:** 31 days prior at 6:00 PM JST.
  - *Difficulty:* Extremely hard. Have a backup plan.
- [ ] **Restaurant Reservations**
  - Book high-end Omakase or Kaiseki dinners (Try [TableCheck](https://www.tablecheck.com/en/japan) or [Omakase](https://omakase.in/en)).
- [ ] **Order eSIM / Pocket Wi-Fi**
  - [Ubigi](https://cellulardata.ubigi.com/) (eSIM) or [Ninja WiFi](https://ninjawifi.com/en) (Physical).

## 🎒 Phase 5: Final Prep (Early October 2026)
*1 Week Out*

- [ ] **[Visit Japan Web](https://vjw-lp.digital.go.jp/en/)**
    -   Complete Immigration & Customs QR codes online.
- [ ] **IC Cards ([Suica](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT207155)/Pasmo)**
    -   Add to Apple Wallet. Load ~5,000 JPY each.
- [ ] **Notify Bank**
    -   Set travel notice (though less required nowadays).
- [ ] **Luggage Strategy**
    -   Pack a "Day 1" backpack. Large bags will be shipped from Tokyo -> Kyoto via [Yamato Transport](https://www.kuronekoyamato.co.jp/ytc/en/).
