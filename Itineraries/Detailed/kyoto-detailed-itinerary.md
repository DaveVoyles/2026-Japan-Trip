# Kyoto Detailed Itinerary

**Schedule:** 06:30 AM - 09:30 PM
**Hotel Hub:** Kyoto Station or Gion/Kawaramachi

##  Day 4: Shinkansen & Foxes (Friday)

Take the Shinkansen to Kyoto and head straight to Fushimi Inari Shrine to hike the Torii gates. Check into the hotel and spend the evening walking through the atmospheric Gion district. Enjoy a traditional Okonomiyaki dinner to wrap up the day.

| Time | Activity | Details | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **07:00** | **Shinkansen** | Depart Tokyo for Kyoto (~2.5 hrs). Buy *Ekiben* (Bento) at station. | $$$ |
| **09:30** | **Arrival** | Arrive Kyoto Station. Drop bags at hotel or coin locker. | - |
| **10:30** | **Fushimi Inari** | **[Fushimi Inari Taisha](https://inari.jp/en/)**. It will be crowded, but hike up to *Yotsutsuji* intersection for views and fewer people. | Free |
| **13:00** | **Lunch** | Udon or Inari Sushi near the shrine. | $ |
| **15:00** | **Check-in** | Head to hotel. Rest for an hour. | - |
| **17:00** | **Gion Walk** | Walk through [Gion district](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3902.html). Spot Geisha (Maiko) if lucky. Respect "No Photo" signs. | - |
| **18:30** | **Dinner** | **Okonomiyaki**.<br>*Recommendation:* **[Gion Tanto](https://tabelog.com/en/kyoto/A2601/A260301/26005066/)** (Teppan grill in tatami setting). | $$ |
| **20:30** | **Night Walk** | Walk along the [Kamo River](https://kyoto.travel/en/nature/kamogawa.html) or [Pontocho Alley](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3921.html). | - |

##  Day 5: Bamboo, Monkeys & Gold (Saturday)

Experience the magic of Arashiyama Bamboo Grove early in the morning, then visit Tenryu-ji Temple. Split up to see monkeys or enjoy riverside coffee before reuniting for the Golden Pavilion. End the day with dinner in Pontocho Alley.

| Time | Activity | Details | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **06:30** | **Arashiyama** | **[Bamboo Grove](https://kyoto.travel/en/nature/bamboogrove.html)**. Arrive early to hear the wind and get empty photos. | Free |
| **08:00** | **Tenryu-ji** | Explore the [temple gardens](https://www.tenryuji.com/en/) right next to the bamboo grove. | $ |
| **09:30** | **SPLIT PATH** | **Dave:** **[Monkey Park Iwatayama](http://www.monkeypark.jp/)** (Hike up to feed monkeys).<br>**Debbie:** **[% Arabica Coffee](https://arabica.coffee/)** (Riverside) or browse Arashiyama shops. | $ |
| **12:00** | **Lunch** | **Tofu / Yuba Cuisine**.<br>*Recommendation:* **[Tousuiro](https://tousuiro.com/en/)** (Tofu Kaiseki) or a local Soba shop. | $$$ |
| **14:00** | **Kinkaku-ji** | **[Golden Pavilion](https://www.shokoku-ji.jp/en/kinkakuji/)**. Take the iconic photo. It's a quick stop (45-60 mins). | $ |
| **16:00** | **Tea Break** | **[Malebranche](https://www.malebranche.co.jp/)** (Kyoto Station) or a cafe for Matcha sweets. | $$ |
| **18:30** | **Dinner** | **[Pontocho Alley](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3921.html)**.<br>Find a restaurant with a river view (if season allows) or cozy atmosphere. | $$$ |
| **20:30** | **Return** | Back to hotel. | - |

##  Day 6: Nara Day Trip & Market (Sunday)

Take a day trip to Nara to feed the bowing deer and see the Giant Buddha at Todai-ji. Watch the famous mochi pounding show before returning to Kyoto for afternoon shopping at Nishiki Market or gaming stores. Conclude with a ramen dinner at Kyoto Station.

| Time | Activity | Details | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **07:30** | **Train to Nara** | Take Kintetsu Line (faster) or JR to Nara (~45 mins). | $ |
| **08:30** | **Nara Park** | **[Deer & Todai-ji](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e4103.html)**. Feed the bowing deer (watch your pockets!). See the [Giant Buddha](https://www.todaiji.or.jp/en/). | $ |
| **11:00** | **Mochi** | **[Nakatanidou](http://www.nakatanidou.jp/)**. Watch the high-speed mochi pounding show. Eat fresh mochi. | $ |
| **12:30** | **Lunch** | Lunch in Nara (Kakinoha Sushi or Udon). | $$ |
| **14:00** | **Return** | Train back to Kyoto. | $ |
| **15:00** | **SPLIT PATH** | **Debbie:** **[Nishiki Market](https://www.kyoto-nishiki.or.jp/)** ("Kyoto's Kitchen"). Soy milk donuts, skewers, knives.<br>**Dave:** **Gaming Stores**. Check *[Surugaya](https://www.suruga-ya.jp/)* or *[Book Off](https://www.bookoff.co.jp/)* for retro finds. | - |
| **18:00** | **Dinner** | **[Kyoto Ramen Koji](https://www.kyoto-ramen-koji.com/english/)** (Station, 10th Floor).<br>Choose from 8 famous ramen shops. | $ |
| **20:00** | **Packing** | Prepare for travel to Osaka tomorrow. | - |
