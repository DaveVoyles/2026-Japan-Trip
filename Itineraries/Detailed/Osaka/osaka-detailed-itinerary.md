# Osaka Detailed Itinerary

The final leg of your trip brings you to Osaka, Japan's energetic culinary capital and home to world-class entertainment.

> For deep-dive details on neighborhoods, logistics, and cultural etiquette, see the [Osaka Neighborhoods & Reference Guide](neighborhoods.md).

**Schedule:** 06:00 AM - 09:30 PM
**Hotel Hub:** Namba or near USJ

**Quick Logistics:**

- **Kyoto Osaka:** JR Special Rapid (30 min).
- **Osaka USJ:** JR Yumesaki Line to Universal City Station (15 min).
- **USJ Namba:** JR to Osaka Station, transfer to Midosuji Line (30-40 min).

**Sightseeing Details:**

- **USJ Strategy:** Be at the gates 45-60 min before opening. Use the USJ App to get "Area Timed Entry" for Nintendo World.
- **Super Nintendo World:** Priority attractions are Mario Kart: Koopa's Challenge and Donkey Kong Country: Mine Cart Madness.
- **Kinopio's Cafe:** Get a timed entry ticket for the restaurant as soon as you enter Nintendo World.
- **Dotonbori:** Iconic neon heart of Osaka. See the Glico Running Man and eat street food like Takoyaki.
- **Kushikatsu:** Deep-fried skewers. Remember: "No double dipping" in the communal sauce!

**Quick Logistics:**

- **Route:** Namba (Subway) -> Shin-Osaka (Shinkansen) -> Shinagawa (Transfer) -> Haneda Airport.
- **Total Transit Time:** Allow 4 hours door-to-terminal to be safe.
- **Lunch:** Buy "Ekiben" (Bento boxes) and 551 Horai buns at Shin-Osaka station to eat on the train using your tray table.

**Sightseeing Details:**

- **Dave (Den Den Town):** A final sweep for retro games at [Super Potato Osaka](https://www.superpotato.com/) before leaving.
- **Debbie (Kuromon Market):** "Osaka's Kitchen" - grab some strawberries or grilled crab legs for a pre-train snack.
- **551 Horai:** The line at Shin-Osaka station is long but moves fast. Get the pork buns!
- **Departure:** United Flight UA130 leaves at 5:45 PM. Arriving at HND by 3:30 PM is perfect.
