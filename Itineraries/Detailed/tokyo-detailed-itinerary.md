# Tokyo Detailed Itinerary

**Schedule:** 06:30 AM - 09:30 PM
**Hotel Hub:** Ginza or Shinjuku
**Duration:** 4 Days (Extended to include Ghibli Museum)

## 🛬 Day 1: Arrival & Reset (Tuesday)

Arrive in Tokyo and settle into the hotel in Ginza or Shinjuku. Take a light evening walk to explore local arcades or basement food halls to get acclimated. End the day with a casual Izakaya dinner before getting an early night's sleep to adjust to the time zone.

| Time | Activity | Details | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **14:00** | **Arrival** | Land at NRT/HND. Pick up Pocket Wi-Fi/Sim if needed. Take Narita Express or Limousine Bus to Hotel. | $$ |
| **16:00** | **Check-in** | Drop bags at hotel in Ginza/Shinjuku. Freshen up. | - |
| **17:00** | **Light Walk** | **Dave:** Visit *[Taito Station](https://www.taito.co.jp/en/gc)* (Arcade) to check out claw machines.<br>**Debbie:** Visit a *[Depachika](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e2070.html)* ([Isetan Shinjuku](https://www.mistore.jp/store/shinjuku/foreign_customer.html) or [Ginza Mitsukoshi](https://www.mistore.jp/store/ginza/foreign_customer.html)) to see the basement food halls. | $ |
| **19:00** | **Dinner** | **[Izakaya](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e2036.html)** (Japanese Pub).<br>*Recommendation:* A local spot near the hotel. Order Yakitori (skewers), Karaage (fried chicken), and Edamame. | $$ |
| **21:00** | **Bed** | Force sleep to adjust to time zone. | - |

Start early at Senso-ji Temple to beat the crowds, then explore Nakamise Street. Split up in the afternoon for retro gaming in Akihabara and kitchenware shopping in Kappabashi. Regroup for a Tonkatsu dinner in the evening.

| **08:00** | **Breakfast** | Coffee & Toast at a nearby *Kissaten* (Retro Coffee Shop). | $ |
| **09:00** | **Nakamise St.** | [Shops open](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3004.html). Buy fresh *Melonpan* (sweet bread) and souvenirs. | $ |
| **10:30** | **SPLIT PATH** | **Dave:** Head to **[Akihabara](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3003.html)**.<br>- *[Super Potato](https://www.superpotato.com/):* Retro games.<br>- *[BEEP](https://www.akihabara-beep.com/):* Vintage PC stuff.<br>- *[Taito Station](https://www.taito.co.jp/en/gc):* Rhythm games.<br><br>**Debbie:** Head to **[Kappabashi](https://www.kappabashi.or.jp/en/)** or **[Ueno](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3019.html)**.<br>- *Kappabashi:* Plastic food samples & kitchenware.<br>- *Ueno Park:* Museums or Zoo. | $$ |
| **13:00** | **Lunch** | **Dave:** Ramen (try *[Afuri](https://afuri.com/)* or *[Ginza Kagari](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1301/A130101/13153231/)*).<br>**Debbie:** Tempura (try *[Tempura Abe](https://abe-ginza.com/)* in Ginza). | $$ |

| **16:00** | **Explore** | Walk through **[Ginza](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3005.html)** (if staying there) or **[Shinjuku](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3011.html)** streets. Visit *[Itoya](https://www.ito-ya.co.jp/en/)* (Stationery) or *[Loft](https://www.loft.co.jp/en/)*. | - |
| **18:30** | **Dinner** | **Tonkatsu** (Pork Cutlet).<br>*Recommendation:* **[Katsukura](https://www.katsukura.jp/en/)** (Shinjuku) or **[Rengatei](http://ginzarengatei.com/)** (Ginza). | $$ |

## 🎬 Day 4: Ghibli Magic & Kichijoji (Friday)

Begin your extended Tokyo stay with a pilgrimage to the Studio Ghibli Museum in Mitaka, surrounded by the beautiful Inokashira Park. Experience exclusive Ghibli short films and immerse yourself in the world of Miyazaki's beloved animations. Spend the afternoon exploring the charming Kichijoji neighborhood with its retro shops, autumn scenery, and cozy cafes.

| Time | Activity | Details | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
