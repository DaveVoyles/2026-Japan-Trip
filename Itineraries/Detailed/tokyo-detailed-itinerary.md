# Tokyo Detailed Itinerary

**Schedule:** 06:30 AM - 09:30 PM
**Hotel Hub:** Ginza or Shinjuku
**Duration:** 4 Days (Extended to include Ghibli Museum)

## 🛬 Day 1: Arrival & Reset (Tuesday)

Arrive in Tokyo and settle into the hotel in Ginza or Shinjuku. Take a light evening walk to explore local arcades or basement food halls to get acclimated. End the day with a casual Izakaya dinner before getting an early night's sleep to adjust to the time zone.

**🚇 Airport Transportation:**
- **From Narita (NRT):**
  - [Narita Express (N'EX)](https://www.jreast.co.jp/e/nex/) to Tokyo/Shinjuku: 60-90 min, ¥3,070-4,070 ($20-27)
  - [Keisei Skyliner](https://www.keisei.co.jp/keisei/tetudou/skyliner/e-skyliner/) to Ueno: 41 min, ¥2,520 ($17)
  - [Airport Limousine Bus](https://website.limousinebus.co.jp/en/) to major hotels: 90-120 min, ¥3,200 ($21)
- **From Haneda (HND):**
  - [Tokyo Monorail](http://www.tokyo-monorail.co.jp/english/) to Hamamatsucho: 20 min, ¥500 ($3)
  - [Keikyu Line](https://www.haneda-tokyo-access.com/en/) to Shinagawa: 14 min, ¥300 ($2)
  - Taxi to Ginza: 20-30 min, ¥5,000-7,000 ($33-47)

**💡 First-Timer Tips:**
- **IC Card:** Buy Suica/PASMO at airport station machines (¥2,000 = ¥500 deposit + ¥1,500 credit) or add to [Apple Wallet](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT207155) before arrival
- **WiFi:** Activate eSIM at airport or pick up pocket WiFi rental
- **ATM:** 7-Eleven or Japan Post Bank ATMs accept foreign cards (usually ¥220/$1.50 fee)
- **Luggage:** Coin lockers at major stations (¥300-700/$2-5) or hotel luggage forwarding available

## ⛩️ Day 2: Old Tokyo & Akihabara (Wednesday)

Start early at Senso-ji Temple to beat the crowds, then explore Nakamise Street. Split up in the afternoon for retro gaming in Akihabara and kitchenware shopping in Kappabashi. Regroup for a Tonkatsu dinner in the evening.

**🚇 Transportation:**
- **To Asakusa:** Ginza Line to Asakusa Station (Exit 1), 20-30 min from most hotels, ¥180-200 ($1.20-1.35)
- **Asakusa → Akihabara:** Tsukuba Express (2 min) or Ginza Line to Ueno, transfer to JR (15 min total)
- **Asakusa → Kappabashi:** 15 min walk west (flat, easy) or Ginza Line to Tawaramachi Station

**💡 First-Timer Tips:**
- **Early Morning:** Senso-ji main hall opens 6 AM; shops open 9 AM
- **Photos:** NO photography inside temple buildings; courtyard is OK
- **Coin Lockers:** Available at Asakusa Station (¥300-600) if carrying shopping bags
- **English:** Limited at small shops; Google Translate camera very helpful
- **Akihabara:** 4-6 floors per building; elevators at back; take your time

## 🌸 Day 3: Pop Culture & Fashion (Thursday)

Begin with a peaceful morning walk at Meiji Shrine followed by fluffy pancakes in Harajuku. Spend the afternoon separately exploring pop culture at Shibuya Parco or floral cafes in Aoyama. Reunite for sunset views at Shibuya Sky and a Yakiniku dinner.

**🚇 Transportation:**
- **To Meiji Shrine:** JR Yamanote Line to Harajuku Station (Omotesando Exit) or Meiji-jingumae Station (Tokyo Metro)
- **Harajuku → Shibuya:** 1 stop on JR Yamanote Line (3 min) or 15 min walk through Omotesando
- **Peak Hours:** Avoid 8-9 AM and 5-7 PM on trains if possible

**💡 First-Timer Tips:**
- **Shrine Etiquette:** Bow before entering torii gate; walk on sides (center for spirits)
- **Photos:** OK in shrine grounds; NO flash in main hall
- **Harajuku Crowds:** Takeshita Street packed 11 AM-5 PM on weekends; go early or explore side streets
- **Shibuya Crossing:** Best photo spot: Starbucks 2F window or Shibuya Sky

## 🎬 Day 4: Ghibli Magic & Kichijoji (Friday)

Begin your extended Tokyo stay with a pilgrimage to the Studio Ghibli Museum in Mitaka, surrounded by the beautiful Inokashira Park. Experience exclusive Ghibli short films and immerse yourself in the world of Miyazaki's beloved animations. Spend the afternoon exploring the charming Kichijoji neighborhood with its retro shops, autumn scenery, and cozy cafes.

**🚇 Transportation:**
- **To Mitaka:** JR Chuo Line (Rapid) from Shinjuku to Mitaka Station (20 min, ¥230/$1.53)
- **Mitaka → Museum:** [Community Bus](https://www.ghibli-museum.jp/en/about-us/map-information/) (10 min, ¥210/$1.40) OR 15 min walk through park
- **Return:** JR Chuo Line from Kichijoji or Mitaka back to Shinjuku

**💡 First-Timer Tips:**
- **CRITICAL:** Museum tickets MUST be pre-booked - [Book here](https://l-tike.com/ghibli/en/) on 10th of month at 10 AM JST, 2 months ahead
- **Tickets Sell Out:** Within minutes to hours; have backup plan ready
- **No Photos Inside:** Only rooftop/exterior; staff will enforce this
- **Timed Entry:** Must arrive within 30 min of your time slot (10 AM, 12 PM, 2 PM, or 4 PM)
- **Duration:** Plan 2-3 hours minimum; no re-entry once you leave
- **English:** All major exhibits have English descriptions
