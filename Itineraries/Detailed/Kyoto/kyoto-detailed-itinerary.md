**Quick Logistics:**

- **Tokyo  Kyoto:** Nozomi Shinkansen (2 hrs 15 min). Sit on **RIGHT (E seat)** for Mt. Fuji.
- **Kyoto Station  Fushimi Inari:** JR Nara Line to Inari Station (5 min).
- **Ekiben:** Buy a famous station bento at Tokyo Station for the ride.

**Sightseeing Details:**

- **Fushimi Inari:** Thousands of vermillion Torii gates. Hike to the "Yotsutsuji" intersection (60-90 min) for great views.
- **Gion:** The historic geisha district. Walk along the Shirakawa canal for the best atmosphere.
- **Pontocho Alley:** A narrow, lantern-lit alley packed with bars and restaurants along the river.

**Quick Logistics:**

- **Kyoto  Nara:** Kintetsu Nara Line (35 min). Kintetsu station is closer to the deer!
- **Nara  Kyoto:** Return via Kintetsu or JR.

**Sightseeing Details:**

- **Nara Park:** Home to 1,000+ sacred deer. Buy "deer crackers" to feed them (they will bow!).
- **Todai-ji:** Houses one of the world's largest bronze Buddha statues (15m tall).
- **Nishiki Market:** "Kyoto's Kitchen" with 100+ stalls selling snacks, pickles, and fresh seafood.
- **Dave (Kyoto Gaming):** Visit [Surugaya Kyoto](https://www.suruga-ya.jp/) and [Book Off] for retro finds.
