# Luggage Forwarding (Takkyubin) Guide

**The Secret to Stress-Free Travel:**
In Japan, you do _not_ drag large suitcases onto Shinkansen trains. You forward them.

## How It Works (Yamato Transport / Kuroneko)

- **Service:** "Takkyubin" (Black Cat).
- **Cost:** ~ - USD per bag.
- **Speed:** Next-day delivery (usually).
- **Reliability:** 99.9%. It is a national standard.

## The Strategy

### Leg 1: Tokyo -> Kyoto

1. **Pack a Day Pack:** Clothes for 1 night + toiletries.
2. **Send Large Bags:** Ask your Tokyo hotel front desk to send your bags to your Kyoto hotel.
   - _Timing:_ Send them the morning _before_ you leave (or 2 days before if you want to be safe).
   - _Result:_ You travel to Kyoto with just a backpack. Your bags are waiting in your room when you arrive.

### Leg 2: Kyoto -> Osaka

- **Skip Forwarding:** It's a short 30-min train ride. Just take your bags.

### Leg 3: Osaka -> Airport (HND)

- **Advice:** **Do NOT forward.** You usually need to send bags 2 days early.
- **Plan:** Take bags on Shinkansen to Tokyo. Book "Oversized Baggage" seats.

## How to Request It

Show this to the hotel front desk:

> **荷物を次のホテルに送りたいです。**
> _(Nimotsu o tsugi no hoteru ni okuritai desu.)_
> "I would like to send my luggage to the next hotel."

## What You Need

1. **Address of Next Hotel:** Have the booking confirmation ready (English is usually fine, Japanese address is better).
2. **Cash:** It is usually paid in cash at the front desk.
