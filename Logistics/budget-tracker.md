## 💡 Money Management Tips

### 🏦 Cash Strategy

- **Withdraw Strategy:** Use 7-Eleven ATMs (accept US debit cards, English menu)
- **Initial Withdrawal:** ¥30,000-¥50,000 ($200-$333) on arrival
- **Daily Cash Needs:** ¥5,000-¥10,000 ($33-$67) for small vendors, shrines, street food
- **Keep Coins:** Japan uses ¥1, ¥5, ¥10, ¥50, ¥100, ¥500 coins extensively - bring a coin purse!

### 💳 Card Usage

- **Credit Cards:** Widely accepted in hotels, department stores, chain restaurants
- **Suica/PASMO:** Essential for trains, subways, konbini, vending machines, coin lockers
- **Load Amount:** Start with ¥5,000 ($33), reload as needed at station machines
- **Apple Pay/Google Pay:** Works with contactless readers (increasing availability)

### 📊 Tracking Tools

- **Apps:** Use Splitwise or Trail Wallet for expense tracking
- **Receipts:** Keep all receipts in envelope by day for post-trip reconciliation
- **Tax-Free Shopping:** Track purchases over ¥5,500 for 10% tax refund

### 🚨 Emergency Fund Access

- **Emergency Cash:** Keep $200 USD in hotel safe as backup
- **Credit Card:** Notify bank of Japan travel dates to avoid fraud blocks
- **ATM Locations:** Mark 7-Eleven locations near hotels in Google Maps offline

## 📈 Budget Optimization Strategies

### 🎯 Save Money On

- **Breakfast:** Konbini meals ($5-10 vs $20-30 at hotels)
- **Lunch:** Lunch sets at restaurants (often 30-50% cheaper than dinner)
- **Transport:** Consider JR Pass only if making multiple long trips (calculate first!)
- **Shopping:** Tax-free purchases, Donki (Don Quijote) for snacks and souvenirs
- **Apps:** Download restaurant/shopping discount apps like [Hot Pepper](https://www.hotpepper.jp/)

### 💰 Worth Splurging On

- **USJ Express Pass:** Skip 2-4 hour lines, maximize park time
- **One Kaiseki Dinner:** Experience traditional multi-course meal (~$100-150 per person)
- **Hotel Location:** Central locations save time and money on transport
- **Luggage Forwarding:** $20 to avoid hauling bags on trains = priceless
