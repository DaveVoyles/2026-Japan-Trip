## 🆘 Lost Passport Procedure

1. **Report to Local Police (Koban)** - Get a police report (essential for embassy)
2. **Call US Embassy/Consulate** - Schedule appointment (usually same/next day for emergency)
3. **Bring to Embassy:**
   - Police report
   - Passport photocopy (if you have it)
   - 2 passport photos (can get at photo booth in Japan)
   - Payment for emergency passport (cash or credit)
   - Proof of citizenship (driver's license, birth certificate copy if available)
4. **Emergency Passport:** Issued same day, valid for limited time to return to US
5. **Cost:** ~$145 USD for emergency passport

## 🏩 What to Bring to Hospital

- ✅ **Passport** (they need this for ID)
- ✅ **Travel Insurance Card** (if you have it)
- ✅ **Credit Card** (most accept Visa/Mastercard)
- ✅ **Cash** (¥20,000-30,000 for emergencies)
- ✅ **List of Medications** (English names + dosages)
- ✅ **Allergy List** (written in English and Japanese if possible)
- ✅ **Hotel Business Card** (for address if you need taxi back)

## 🚑 What to Expect at Emergency Room

1. **Registration Desk:** Show passport, explain problem (use translation app)
2. **Insurance/Payment:** They may ask for deposit (¥10,000-20,000 cash)
3. **Triage:** Nurse checks vitals, rates urgency
4. **Wait:** Can be 1-3 hours depending on severity
5. **Doctor Visit:** Interpreter may be available (ask at desk)
6. **Pharmacy:** Usually inside hospital, pick up meds before leaving
7. **Payment:** Pay at exit, get itemized receipt for insurance claim
8. **Insurance Claim:** Save ALL receipts, file claim when back home

## 🚓 Koban (Police Box) Locations

Koban are small police stations found every few blocks in Japan. Officers are friendly and often help with:

- Directions
- Lost items
- Crime reports
- Emergency calls

**Tokyo:** Look for blue lamp outside, usually near major intersections and stations
**Kyoto:** Near Kyoto Station, Gion, major temple areas
**Osaka:** Dotonbori, Namba Station, Umeda Station all have koban nearby

**Lost Item Procedure:** Most items turned in to koban or station lost & found. Check within 24 hours!

## 🌪️ Natural Disaster Guidance

### Earthquake

- **DROP, COVER, HOLD ON** - Get under table/desk
- **Stay Inside** - Don't run outside during shaking
- **After:** Check for injuries, watch for aftershocks
- **Tsunami:** If near coast and magnitude >7.0, head to high ground IMMEDIATELY
- **App:** "Safety Tips" gives early warning (download before trip)

### Typhoon (June is the start of the season)

- **Stay Indoors** - Avoid going out during typhoon or heavy rainy season storms
- **Watch News:** NHK World Japan (English channel)
- **Transport:** Trains/flights may be cancelled/delayed during extreme rain, don't fight it
