#  Tech Setup & App Guide

**Goal:** Set up your phone *before* you get on the plane.

##  1. Suica / PASMO (Transit Card)

**Why:** Pays for trains, subways, vending machines, and konbini snacks.
**Device:** iPhone (Apple Wallet).

### Setup Steps (Do this NOW):
1. Open **Apple Wallet**.
2. Tap **+** (Add Card).
3. Select **Transit Card**.
4. Search for **"Suica"** or **"PASMO"** (Either works fine).
5. Add money (Load 1,000 to start). *Note: Visa sometimes fails. Mastercard/Amex work best.*
6. **Express Transit Mode:** Go to Settings -> Wallet & Apple Pay -> Express Transit Card -> Select Suica.
   - *Result:* You don't need FaceID to tap through gates. Just tap your locked phone.

##  2. SmartEx (Shinkansen)

**Why:** Buy bullet train tickets on your phone and link them to your Suica.
**App:** "SmartEx" (Central Japan Railway).

### Setup Steps:
1. Download **SmartEx App**.
2. Register an account.
3. **Link Credit Card:** Add your travel card.
4. **Link IC Card:**
   - Open Apple Wallet -> Tap Suica -> Tap 'i' or '...' -> Copy **IC Card Number**.
   - Paste this number into SmartEx.
   - *Result:* When you book a Shinkansen, you just tap your phone at the Shinkansen gate. No paper ticket!

##  3. eSIM (Data)

**Why:** Cheaper and faster than carrier roaming.
**App:** **Ubigi** or **Airalo**.

### Setup Steps (1 Week Before Trip):
1. Download **Ubigi**.
2. Create account.
3. Buy a **Japan Data Plan** (e.g., 10GB for ~).
4. **Install eSIM:** Follow the prompts to add the eSIM profile to your iPhone settings.
5. **On Arrival:** Go to Settings -> Cellular -> Turn ON the Ubigi line and turn OFF your primary line's data roaming.

##  4. Google Maps

**Why:** Navigation is life.

### Setup Steps:
1. **Offline Maps:** Download "Tokyo", "Kyoto", and "Osaka" areas for offline use (saves data/battery).
2. **Save Locations:** Star your hotels and key spots so they are easy to find.

##  5. Translation

**Why:** Reading menus and signs.

### Setup Steps:
1. **Google Translate:**
   - Download **Japanese** language pack for offline use.
   - Test the **Camera Tool** (Google Lens) on a Japanese product label at home.
2. **DeepL:**
   - Better for typing/text translation. Download the app.

##  6. Universal Studios Japan

**Why:** Nintendo World entry.

### Setup Steps:
1. Download **USJ Official App**.
2. **Register Ticket:** Scan your park ticket QR code into the app *before* you enter the park (or right at the gate).
3. **Practice:** Learn where the "Area Timed Entry" button is. You will need to tap this the second you walk through the gates.
