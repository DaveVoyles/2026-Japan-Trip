# Tokyo Ticketing & Reservation Guide

This guide provides essential details for securing tickets and reservations for the attractions and dining spots listed in the Tokyo itinerary.

## 🎟️ Key Attractions (Advance Booking Required)

### 1. Studio Ghibli Museum (Mitaka)
*   **Booking Window:** Tickets go on sale on the **10th of each month at 10:00 AM JST** for the following month.
*   **How to Book:** Use the official [Lawson Ticket English Site](https://l-tike.com/st1/ghibli-en/).
*   **Crucial Tips:** 
    *   Tickets sell out within minutes. Be ready the moment they go live.
    *   You must provide passport details for the lead booker.
    *   Entry is for a specific time slot (e.g., 10:00 AM).

### 2. Shibuya Sky
*   **Booking Window:** Tickets can be booked up to **4 weeks (1 month) in advance**.
*   **How to Book:** [Official Shibuya Sky Website](https://www.shibuya-scramble-square.com/sky/ticket/).
*   **Crucial Tips:** 
    *   Sunset is the most popular time. Book exactly 4 weeks out if you want a sunset slot.
    *   Online tickets are cheaper than walk-up tickets (¥2,200 vs ¥2,500).

## 🎮 Gaming & Pop Culture (Same-Day / On-Site)

### 1. Nintendo Tokyo & Capcom Store (Shibuya Parco 6F)
*   **Reservation:** No advance online booking.
*   **System:** On busy days (weekends/holidays/new releases), they use **Numbered Entry Tickets (Seiriken)**.
*   **How to Get In:** 
    *   Staff distribute paper tickets with a return time.
    *   Distribution usually starts at the **Spain-Zaka entrance** of Shibuya Parco before 10:00 AM.
    *   Arrive by 9:00 AM or 9:30 AM to ensure an early slot.

### 2. Pokémon Center Shibuya (Shibuya Parco 6F)
*   **System:** Generally walk-in, but follows the same "numbered ticket" system as Nintendo if the floor is overcrowded.
*   **Pokémon Design Lab:** If you want to design a custom T-shirt, head there first as there is often a 60-90 minute wait for printing.

## 🍽️ Dining Reservations

### 1. A Happy Pancake (Harajuku/Shibuya)
*   **Booking Window:** Online reservations open **2 weeks in advance** at midnight JST.
*   **How to Book:** [Official Reservation Site](https://magia.tokyo/reserve/?lang=en).
*   **Crucial Tips:** Weekends are extremely busy; booking is highly recommended to avoid 2+ hour wait times.

### 2. Jojoen (Yakiniku)
*   **Booking Window:** Reservations recommended at least **1-2 weeks in advance**, especially for dinner.
*   **How to Book:** Through their official website or hotel concierge.
*   **Crucial Tips:** Ask for a "window seat" or "private room" if available at the Shinjuku/Shibuya branches for the best experience.

---

## 🏯 Free Attractions (No Tickets Needed)

*   **Senso-ji Temple (Asakusa):** Always open and free. Arrive before 8:00 AM to avoid the main crowds.
*   **Meiji Shrine (Harajuku):** Free entry. Opens at sunrise, closes at sunset.
*   **Inokashira Park (Mitaka/Kichijoji):** Free public park.
*   **Nakano Broadway / Akihabara Shops:** Free to enter and browse.

---

## 🎒 Logistics Reminders

*   **Suica / Pasmo / ICOCA:** Ensure your digital transit card is loaded on your phone for seamless travel on the JR Chuo Line and Tokyo Metro.
*   **Yamato Transport (Takkyubin):** Forwarding bags from Tokyo to Kyoto (Day 4) can be done at most hotel front desks or 7-Eleven/FamilyMart stores. Plan for "Next Day" delivery.
