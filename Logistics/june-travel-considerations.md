## 📅 Summary: June vs. October

While October offers crisp air and autumn colors, June brings the **Tsuyu (Rainy Season)**.
It is humid, overcast, and wet, but significantly cheaper and less crowded than the peak autumn months.

## 🌦️ Weather & Comfort (The "Tsuyu" Factor)

- **The Rain:** It doesn't usually pour all day, but it is persistent, "grey," and very humid (80%+).
- **Temperature:** 20-27°C (68-81°F). Because of humidity, it feels much warmer than the thermometer says.
- **Packing Needs:**
  - High-quality light rain shell (breathable).
  - Waterproof shoes or quick-dry sneakers.
  - Anti-frizz hair products (the humidity is real!).
  - Portable fan / cooling towels.

---

## 🎡 Crowds & Tourism

- **General:** June is one of the quietest months for international tourism.
  You will face significantly shorter lines at temples in Kyoto and popular restaurants in Tokyo compared to October.
- **USJ / Disney:** Still busy with locals, but without the international autumn crowds.
  Early June (weekdays) is a "Goldilocks" zone for lower wait times.
- **Public Holidays:** None in June! This is a huge benefit—no "Golden Week" or "Obon" domestic travel spikes to compete with.

---

## 🏮 Events & Highlights (June 2026)

1. **Sanno Matsuri (Tokyo) ⭐:**
   - **Wait for it:** This is one of Tokyo's three major festivals. It only happens in **even-numbered years**.
   - **2026 is an ON year.** Usually held mid-June (June 7-17).
     Expect a massive parade with portable shrines (mikoshi) and traditional costumes near Hie Shrine.
2. **Hydrangea (Ajisai) Season:**
   - Temples like **Meigetsu-in** in Kamakura or **Mimuroto-ji** in Kyoto become stunning with blue and purple blooms.
     This only happens in June.
3. **Firefly Watching (Hotaru):**
   - Early June is the peak for fireflies in rural areas or Japanese gardens (like Chinzan-so in Tokyo).

## 💰 Budget & Costs

- **Flights:** Expect to save $200-$400 per person vs. October.
- **Hotels:** Many luxury hotels offer "Rainy Season" discounts.
  You can likely upgrade your room for the same price as a standard room in October.

## 🏁 The Verdict: Should you go in June?

- **Go if:** You want to save money, hate long lines, and don't mind carrying an umbrella. The Sanno Matsuri is a rare treat.
- **Avoid if:** You hate humidity or want those "clean" blue-sky Japan photos. October is objectively "nicer" weather-wise.
