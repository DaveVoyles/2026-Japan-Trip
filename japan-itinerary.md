# Japan Trip Itinerary - October 2026

## Overview

- **Dates:** October 2026 (Target: Mid-October for pleasant weather/early autumn leaves)
- **Duration:** 8 Days (Consider extending to 10 if possible)
- **Travelers:** Dave (Gaming, Foxes, Temples, Sushi) & Wife (Flowers, Baked Goods, Non-Sushi Food)
- **Route:** Tokyo (3 Nights) -> Kyoto (3 Nights) -> Osaka (1 Night) -> Fly Out

## Strategy: "Together & Apart"

To balance interests, some afternoons feature "Split Paths" where you pursue separate hobbies nearby and meet up for dinner.

---

## Day 1: Arrival in Tokyo (Tuesday)

- **Morning/Afternoon:** Arrive NRT/HND from Newark (EWR).
- **Transfer:** Hotel in **Ginza** or **Shinjuku** (Ginza is better for Wife's shopping/bakeries; Shinjuku better for Dave's VR/Nightlife).
- **Evening:** Light walk.
  - *Dave:* Check out a Taito Station arcade.
  - *Wife:* Visit a "Depachika" (Department Store Basement) for high-end baked goods/fruit.
- **Dinner:** Izakaya (Japanese Pub) - offers variety for both (Yakitori, fried foods, sashimi).

## Day 2: Old Tokyo & The "Split" (Wednesday)

- **Morning (Together):** **Senso-ji Temple (Asakusa)**.
  - *Eat:* Fresh melonpan (sweet bread) on Nakamise Street.
- **Afternoon (Split Path):**
  - *Dave:* **Akihabara** (10 min subway). Super Potato, Mandarake, Retro gaming.
  - *Wife:* **Kappabashi Kitchen Town** (plastic food samples, ceramics) OR **Ueno Park Museums**.
- **Late Afternoon (Together):** Meet in Akihabara or Ueno.
- **Dinner:** Tonkatsu (Breaded Pork Cutlet) - safe for non-sushi eaters, delicious.

## Day 3: Pop Culture & Flowers (Thursday)

- **Morning (Together):** **Meiji Shrine** (Forest in the city).
- **Lunch:** Harajuku area.
- **Afternoon (Split Path):**
  - *Dave:* **Shibuya Parco** (Nintendo Tokyo, Capcom Store, Pokemon Center).
  - *Wife:* **Aoyama Flower Market Tea House** (Focus on the *Flowers* and ambience, or visit nearby bakeries in Omotesando).
- **Evening (Together):** **Shibuya Crossing** & Hachiko Statue.
- **Dinner:** Yakiniku (Japanese BBQ) - grill your own meat/veggies.

## Day 4: Bullet Train to Foxes (Friday)

- **Morning:** Shinkansen to Kyoto (~2.5 hrs). Buy "Ekiben" (station bento) - huge variety.
- **Afternoon (Together):** **Fushimi Inari Shrine**.
  - *Dave:* Thousands of Torii gates + Fox statues (Kitsune).
  - *Note:* Real foxes are rare here; this is spiritual.
- **Late Afternoon:** Check into Kyoto Hotel.
- **Dinner:** Gion District. Try **Okonomiyaki** (Savory pancake) or Tempura.

## Day 5: Bamboo & Gold (Saturday)

- **Morning (Together):** **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove**.
  - *Dave:* **Monkey Park Iwatayama** (hike up, see monkeys).
  - *Wife:* **Tenryu-ji Temple Gardens** or % Arabica Coffee (famous riverside cafe).
- **Lunch:** Tofu/Yuba cuisine (Kyoto specialty) or Soba noodles.
- **Afternoon (Together):** **Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)**.
- **Evening:** Pontocho Alley (atmospheric dining by the river).

## Day 6: Deer & Shopping (Sunday)

- **Morning (Together):** Train to **Nara Park** (45 min).
  - *Activity:* Bowing deer, Todai-ji Temple (Giant Buddha).
- **Afternoon (The Compromise):** Return to Kyoto.
  - *Wife:* **Nishiki Market** ("Kyoto's Kitchen") for snacks/shopping or **Kyoto Botanical Gardens**.
  - *Dave:* **Kyoto Gaming Stores** (Surugaya, Book Off) or relax.
- **Dinner:** Kyoto Station area (Ramen Street).

## Day 7: Osaka Energy (Monday)

- **Morning:** Train to Osaka (30 min). Drop bags.
- **Option A (Together):** **Universal Studios Japan** (Super Nintendo World).
  - *Warning:* Requires Express Pass bought months ahead. Very crowded.
- **Option B (City Split):**
  - *Dave:* **Den Den Town** (Osaka's Akihabara - retro gaming/anime).
  - *Wife:* **Dotonbori/Shinsaibashi** shopping arcades (Stationery, Cosmetics).
- **Evening:** **Dotonbori** neon lights.
- **Dinner:** Kushikatsu (Deep fried skewers) - iconic Osaka food.

## Day 8: Departure (Tuesday)

- **Morning:** Last minute shopping.
  - *Wife:* Don Quijote for snacks/cosmetics.
- **Afternoon:** Transfer to KIX (Kansai Airport) or Shinkansen back to Tokyo (NRT/HND) depending on flight.
- **Flight:** EWR (Newark).

## Notes on October

- **Weather:** Highs 65-75F (18-24C). Perfect for walking.
- **Flowers:** Cosmos flowers (Showa Kinen Park - Tokyo), Roses, early Autumn foliage in mountains.
- **Events:** Jidai Matsuri (Kyoto, Oct 22) - massive historical parade.
