# Japan Food Guide (Non-Sushi Focus)

## Overview
- **Focus:** High-quality non-sushi options for Debbie (Ramen, Tempura, Okonomiyaki, Yakiniku, Bakeries).
- **Budget:** Breakfast/Lunch < USD (7,500); Dinner < USD (22,500).
- **Locations:** Aligned with hotel hubs (Ginza, Kyoto Station, Namba).

---

##  Tokyo (Ginza & Shinjuku)

### Ramen & Noodles
- **Ginza Kagari (Ginza):** Famous "Chicken Paitan" (creamy chicken soup). Michelin Bib Gourmand.
  - *Est. Price:* 1,500 - 2,500 ( -  USD).
  - *Note:* Long lines. Go early (11:00 AM) or late.
- **Afuri Ramen (Shinjuku/Harajuku):** Yuzu Shio Ramen. Light, citrusy broth.
  - *Est. Price:* 1,200 - 1,800 ( -  USD).
- **Udon Shin (Shinjuku):** Freshly cut udon. Cult following.
  - *Est. Price:* 1,500 - 2,500 ( -  USD).

### Tempura & Tonkatsu
- **Tempura Abe (Ginza):** High-quality tempura lunch bowls (Tendon) at reasonable prices.
  - *Est. Price:* Lunch 1,500 ( USD); Dinner 6,000 ( USD).
- **Katsukura (Shinjuku):** Kyoto-style Tonkatsu (pork cutlet). Unlimited cabbage/soup.
  - *Est. Price:* 2,000 - 3,500 ( -  USD).

### Bakeries & Sweets
- **Ginza Kimuraya:** The inventor of "Anpan" (red bean bun).
  - *Est. Price:* 200 - 500 (.50 - .50 USD).
- **Centre The Bakery (Ginza):** Famous for "Shokupan" (fluffy white bread) toast sets.
  - *Est. Price:* 1,500 - 3,000 ( -  USD).

### Yakiniku (Japanese BBQ)
- **Jojoen (Multiple Locations):** Upscale, reliable Yakiniku. Great views in some locations.
  - *Est. Price:* Dinner 10,000 - 15,000 ( -  USD).

##  Kyoto (Station & Gion)

### Casual & Comfort
- **Kyoto Ramen Koji (Station, 10th Floor):** "Ramen Street" with 8 famous shops from across Japan.
  - *Est. Price:* 1,000 - 1,500 ( -  USD).
- **Gion Tanto (Gion):** Okonomiyaki and Yakisoba in a tatami setting (some tables available).
  - *Est. Price:* 1,500 - 3,000 ( -  USD).

### Tempura & Tofu
- **Yoshikawa Tempura (Downtown):** Classic tempura counter in an old inn.
  - *Est. Price:* Lunch 4,000 ( USD); Dinner 15,000+ (+ USD).
- **Tousuiro (Gion):** Tofu Kaiseki (multi-course tofu meal). Very Kyoto.
  - *Est. Price:* Lunch 4,000 ( USD).

### Sweets
- **Malebranche (Kyoto Station):** Famous for "Cha no Ka" (Matcha Langue de Chat cookies).
- **Nishiki Market:** "Soy Milk Donuts" and fresh skewers.

##  Osaka (Namba & Dotonbori)

### The "Konamon" (Flour-based food) Capital
- **Mizuno (Dotonbori):** Michelin Bib Gourmand Okonomiyaki. Famous for "Yamaimo" (Yam) pancakes.
  - *Est. Price:* 1,500 - 2,500 ( -  USD).
- **Kushikatsu Daruma (Dotonbori):** Deep-fried skewers. "No double dipping" sauce.
  - *Est. Price:* 2,000 - 4,000 ( -  USD).
- **551 Horai (Everywhere):** Steamed Pork Buns (Butaman). A must-eat snack.
  - *Est. Price:* 400 (.50 USD) for two.

### Sweets
- **Rikuro Ojisan (Namba):** Jiggly Cheesecake. Freshly baked with a bell ringing.
  - *Est. Price:* 900 ( USD) for a whole cake.
