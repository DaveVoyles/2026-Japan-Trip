# Tokyo Detailed Itinerary

**Schedule:** 06:30 AM - 09:30 PM
**Hotel Hub:** Ginza or Shinjuku

##  Day 1: Arrival & Reset (Tuesday)

| Time | Activity | Details | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **14:00** | **Arrival** | Land at NRT/HND. Pick up Pocket Wi-Fi/Sim if needed. Take Narita Express or Limousine Bus to Hotel. | $$ |
| **16:00** | **Check-in** | Drop bags at hotel in Ginza/Shinjuku. Freshen up. | - |
| **17:00** | **Light Walk** | **Dave:** Visit *Taito Station* (Arcade) to check out claw machines.<br>**Debbie:** Visit a *Depachika* (Isetan Shinjuku or Ginza Mitsukoshi) to see the basement food halls. | $ |

##  Day 2: Old Tokyo & Akihabara (Wednesday)

| Time | Activity | Details | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **06:30** | **Senso-ji Temple** | **Start Here.** Beat the crowds at Asakusa's famous temple. Get photos of the Kaminarimon Gate without thousands of people. | Free |
| **08:00** | **Breakfast** | Coffee & Toast at a nearby *Kissaten* (Retro Coffee Shop). | $ |
| **09:00** | **Nakamise St.** | Shops open. Buy fresh *Melonpan* (sweet bread) and souvenirs. | $ |
| **10:30** | **SPLIT PATH** | **Dave:** Head to **Akihabara**.<br>- *Super Potato:* Retro games.<br>- *BEEP:* Vintage PC stuff.<br>- *Taito Station:* Rhythm games.<br><br>**Debbie:** Head to **Kappabashi** or **Ueno**.<br>- *Kappabashi:* Plastic food samples & kitchenware.<br>- *Ueno Park:* Museums or Zoo. | $$ |
| **13:00** | **Lunch** | **Dave:** Ramen (try *Afuri* or *Ginza Kagari*).<br>**Debbie:** Tempura (try *Tempura Abe* in Ginza). | $$ |
| **14:30** | **Regroup** | Meet back at hotel or a central cafe to drop off shopping bags. | - |
| **16:00** | **Explore** | Walk through **Ginza** (if staying there) or **Shinjuku** streets. Visit *Itoya* (Stationery) or *Loft*. | - |
| **18:30** | **Dinner** | **Tonkatsu** (Pork Cutlet).<br>*Recommendation:* **Katsukura** (Shinjuku) or **Rengatei** (Ginza). | $$ |
| **20:30** | **Return** | Back to hotel. Prepare for early morning. | - |
