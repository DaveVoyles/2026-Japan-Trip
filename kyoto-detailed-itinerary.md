# Kyoto Detailed Itinerary

**Schedule:** 06:30 AM - 09:30 PM
**Hotel Hub:** Kyoto Station or Gion/Kawaramachi

##  Day 4: Shinkansen & Foxes (Friday)

| Time | Activity | Details | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **07:00** | **Shinkansen** | Depart Tokyo for Kyoto (~2.5 hrs). Buy *Ekiben* (Bento) at station. | $$$ |
| **09:30** | **Arrival** | Arrive Kyoto Station. Drop bags at hotel or coin locker. | - |
| **10:30** | **Fushimi Inari** | **Fushimi Inari Taisha**. It will be crowded, but hike up to *Yotsutsuji* intersection for views and fewer people. | Free |
| **13:00** | **Lunch** | Udon or Inari Sushi near the shrine. | $ |
| **15:00** | **Check-in** | Head to hotel. Rest for an hour. | - |
| **17:00** | **Gion Walk** | Walk through Gion district. Spot Geisha (Maiko) if lucky. Respect "No Photo" signs. | - |
| **18:30** | **Dinner** | **Okonomiyaki**.<br>*Recommendation:* **Gion Tanto** (Teppan grill in tatami setting). | $$ |
| **20:30** | **Night Walk** | Walk along the Kamo River or Pontocho Alley. | - |

##  Day 5: Bamboo, Monkeys & Gold (Saturday)

| Time | Activity | Details | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **06:30** | **Arashiyama** | **Bamboo Grove**. Arrive early to hear the wind and get empty photos. | Free |
| **08:00** | **Tenryu-ji** | Explore the temple gardens right next to the bamboo grove. | $ |
| **09:30** | **SPLIT PATH** | **Dave:** **Monkey Park Iwatayama** (Hike up to feed monkeys).<br>**Debbie:** **% Arabica Coffee** (Riverside) or browse Arashiyama shops. | $ |
| **12:00** | **Lunch** | **Tofu / Yuba Cuisine**.<br>*Recommendation:* **Tousuiro** (Tofu Kaiseki) or a local Soba shop. | $$-$$$ |
| **14:00** | **Kinkaku-ji** | **Golden Pavilion**. Take the iconic photo. It's a quick stop (45-60 mins). | $ |
| **16:00** | **Tea Break** | **Malebranche** (Kyoto Station) or a cafe for Matcha sweets. | $$ |
| **18:30** | **Dinner** | **Pontocho Alley**.<br>Find a restaurant with a river view (if season allows) or cozy atmosphere. | $$-$$$ |
| **20:30** | **Return** | Back to hotel. | - |

##  Day 6: Nara Day Trip & Market (Sunday)

| Time | Activity | Details | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **07:30** | **Train to Nara** | Take Kintetsu Line (faster) or JR to Nara (~45 mins). | $ |
| **08:30** | **Nara Park** | **Deer & Todai-ji**. Feed the bowing deer (watch your pockets!). See the Giant Buddha. | $ |
| **11:00** | **Mochi** | **Nakatanidou**. Watch the high-speed mochi pounding show. Eat fresh mochi. | $ |
| **12:30** | **Lunch** | Lunch in Nara (Kakinoha Sushi or Udon). | $$ |
| **14:00** | **Return** | Train back to Kyoto. | $ |
| **15:00** | **SPLIT PATH** | **Debbie:** **Nishiki Market** ("Kyoto's Kitchen"). Soy milk donuts, skewers, knives.<br>**Dave:** **Gaming Stores**. Check *Surugaya* or *Book Off* for retro finds. | - |
| **18:00** | **Dinner** | **Kyoto Ramen Koji** (Station, 10th Floor).<br>Choose from 8 famous ramen shops. | $ |
| **20:00** | **Packing** | Prepare for travel to Osaka tomorrow. | - |
