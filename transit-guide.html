🚌 Airport Limousine Bus

What: A public airport shuttle operated by Limousine Bus Co. — not private, despite the name. Just a comfortable coach.
Route: Haneda Airport → Keio Plaza Hotel (Shinjuku). Stops directly at the hotel's front door — no dragging luggage through Shinjuku Station.
Cost: ~¥1,200–1,600/person (~$8–11 USD)
Duration: ~35–50 min from Haneda
How: After customs, follow signs to the Limousine Bus counter in the arrival hall. Buy tickets at the counter or kiosk. Buses to Shinjuku/Keio Plaza run frequently.
⏱️ Day 1 timeline: Land 1:35 PM → customs ~2:15 PM → bus ~2:30 PM → hotel ~3:00 PM (right when check-in opens).

🚅 Shinkansen (Bullet Train)

Your routes:
Day 5 (May 29): Tokyo → Kyoto — Nozomi, Green Car, conf# 2003. Departs 10:00 AM, arrives ~12:15 PM.
Day 9 (Jun 2): Osaka → Tokyo — Nozomi, Green Car, conf# 2005. Departs 2:15 PM, arrives ~6:45 PM.
Ticket TypeCost (One Way)Details
Ordinary Car~¥14,000 ($93)3×2 seating, comfortable, plenty of legroom
Green Car ✅~¥19,000 ($126)2×2 seating, heated seats, quieter, dedicated luggage space
🗻 Mt. Fuji view: Seat E is typically the right-side Fuji-view seat headed from Tokyo to Kyoto. Verify the actual reserved car/seat in SmartEX before departure.
How to book:
1. SmartEx App (Recommended): Link a credit card + your Suica IC card number. Book on your phone, tap your phone at the Shinkansen gate — no paper ticket.
2. Station machines: Green machines marked "Shinkansen" → English → Purchase Ticket → Tokaido Shinkansen. Pay with credit card.
🧳 Baggage 160 Rule: If your luggage total dimensions exceed 160 cm (~62 in), you must reserve a seat with an "Oversized Baggage Area" (usually the last row). Penalty: ¥1,000 fine. Filter for it in SmartEx.

🔄 Final Return: Osaka → Shinagawa → Haneda

The final return is split across two days: Day 9 gets you from Osaka to the Shinagawa hotel, then Day 10 is the airport transfer to Haneda.
1. Day 9: Cross Hotel Osaka → Namba → Shin-Osaka. Board Nozomi #2005 at 2:15 PM.
2. Day 9: Arrive Shinagawa around 6:45 PM and walk to Shinagawa Prince Hotel.
3. Day 10: Leave Shinagawa by about 1:15 PM for Haneda T3. Keikyu Airport Express takes about 15–20 minutes once you're on the platform.
4. Day 10: Target Haneda arrival around 2:30 PM for UA130 at 5:45 PM.
💡 Why Shinagawa? It keeps the final night close to Haneda and avoids a same-day Osaka-to-airport sprint.

💳 IC Cards (Suica)

Your key to the city — works on trains, subways, buses, vending machines, and convenience stores (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart).
Card TypeCostHow to GetNotes
Mobile Suica ✅ Free Apple Wallet → "+" → Transit Card → Suica Top up with Amex/MC (Visa often fails with foreign cards). Guide
Welcome Suica Free Vending machines at Haneda/Narita arrival hall Valid 28 days, no refund on balance. Good phone-dies backup. Info
Usage tips:
• Tap In / Tap Out on the yellow reader at ticket gates.
• Keep ~¥2,000 ($13) loaded. Max balance: ¥20,000 ($133).
• Look for the IC logo at shops and vending machines.

📦 Luggage Forwarding (Takkyubin)

Don't haul heavy bags on the train. Ship them to your next hotel instead.
ServiceCostSpeedHow
Yamato Transport (Black Cat) ~¥2,000–3,500/bag ($15–25) Next day Ask hotel front desk: "Takkyubin, please"
Procedure:
1. Night before: Pack large suitcase. Keep a small backpack with essentials for 1 night.
2. Morning: Bring bags to hotel lobby.
3. Form: Staff help fill out the waybill. You need the address and phone number of your next hotel.
4. Payment: Usually cash only, or charged to room.
5. Arrival: Bags waiting in your room at the next hotel.
