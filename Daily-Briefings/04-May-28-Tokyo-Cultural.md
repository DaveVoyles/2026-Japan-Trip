#  Day 4: Thursday, May 28 (Tokyo: Asakusa & Akihabara)

**Goal:** Old Tokyo in the morning, Cyber Tokyo in the afternoon.

##  Timeline (JST)

- **09:00 AM:** **Senso-ji Temple** (Asakusa).
  - _Do:_ Cleanse hands at the fountain. Draw an "Omikuji" (fortune).
- **11:00 AM:** **Kappabashi (Kitchen Town)**.
  - _Shop:_ Knives (Kama-Asa), Plastic Food samples.
- **01:00 PM:** Lunch: **Tempura Daikokuya** (Asakusa).
- **03:00 PM:** **Akihabara (Electric Town)**.
  - _Target:_ **Super Potato** (Retro Games), **Mandarake Complex**.
  - _Arcade:_ **Taito Station** (Gachapon & Ufo catchers).
- **06:00 PM:** Maid Cafe? Or Kanda Myojin Shrine (Anime shrine).
- **08:00 PM:** Dinner near Shinjuku/Hotel.

##  Rainy Day Option?
- **Akihabara** is surprisingly rain-friendly (big buildings).
- **Asakusa** has a covered arcade (Nakamise-dori), but the temple grounds are exposed.

##  Key Phrase
- "Menzei dekimazu ka?" (Can I do tax-free?)
- "Sumimasen, kore wa doko desu ka?" (Excuse me, where is this? *Show picture of game*)
