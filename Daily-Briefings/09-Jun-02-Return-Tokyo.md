#  Day 9: Tuesday, June 2 (Travel: Osaka -> Tokyo/Haneda)

**Goal:** Osaka Castle, Last Train, Haneda Airport Hotel.

##  Timeline (JST)

- **09:00 AM:** **Osaka Castle**.
  - _Do:_ Don't go inside (it's modern concrete). Walk the grounds.
  - _Photo:_ Go to the observation deck.
- **11:00 AM:** Lunch: **Okonomiyaki** (Pancakes).
  - _Try:_ **Mizuno** in Dotonbori.
- **02:00 PM:** **Shin-Osaka Station**.
  - _Train:_ Shinkansen to Shinagawa or Tokyo.
  - _Seat:_ Right side (E) for Fuji again?
- **04:30 PM:** Arrive Tokyo (Shinagawa Station is easier for Haneda transfer).
- **05:30 PM:** Train to **Haneda Airport Terminal 3**.
- **06:00 PM:** Check in: **Royal Park Hotel The Haneda**.
  - _Location:_ Directly inside the terminal.
- **07:00 PM:** Dinner: **Haneda Airport Garden** (New mall attached to T3).
  - _Eat:_ Last Tonkatsu or Ramen.
  - _Relax:_ **Izumi Tenku no Yu** (Onsen with view of Mt Fuji/Planes) at the top of the mall.
