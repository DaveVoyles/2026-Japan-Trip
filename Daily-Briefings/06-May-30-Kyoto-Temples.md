#  Day 6: Saturday, May 30 (Kyoto: Arashiyama & Gold)

**Goal:** Bamboo forest (early), Golden Pavilion, and the Philosopher's Path.

##  Timeline (JST)

- **07:00 AM:** **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove**.
  - _Why Early?_ Crowds ruin it by 9 AM.
  - _Coffee:_ % Arabica Kyoto Arashiyama (Iconic latte).
- **10:00 AM:** **Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)**.
  - _Transport:_ Taxi from Arashiyama (~25 mins) is faster than bus.
- **12:00 PM:** Lunch: **Okonomiyaki** or **Soba** nearby.
- **02:00 PM:** **Kiyomizu-dera** (The Wooden Stage).
  - _Prepare:_ Huge uphill walk. Wear sneakers.
  - _Shop:_ Sannenzaka/Ninenzaka streets (traditional houses).
- **04:30 PM:** **Starbucks Ninenzaka** (Tatami mat seating inside old house).
- **07:00 PM:** **Kichi Kichi Omurice** (If Reserved!).
  - _Alt:_ Teppanyaki beef.
