#  Day 7: Sunday, May 31 (Travel: Kyoto -> Osaka)

**Goal:** Fushimi Inari (The Red Gates) and Neon Dotonbori.

##  Timeline (JST)

- **07:00 AM:** **Fushimi Inari Taisha**.
  - _Why Early?_ It becomes a zoo by 10 AM.
  - _Hike:_ Go past the first lookout (Yotsutsuji) for empty gates.
- **11:00 AM:** Check out of Cross Hotel Kyoto.
  - _Bags:_ Send to **Cross Hotel Osaka** (Same chain, easy). Or taxi with them.
- **12:00 PM:** Train to Osaka (Keihan Line or JR). ~45 mins.
- **02:00 PM:** Check in **Cross Hotel Osaka** (Dotonbori).
- **03:00 PM:** **Dotonbori Street Food**.
  - _Eat:_ Takoyaki (Octopus Balls) at Acchichi Honpo (by the bridge).
- **06:00 PM:** **Glico Man Sign** (Photo Op).
- **08:00 PM:** **Hozenji Yokocho** (Moss Temple alley).
  - _Drink:_ Old-school Izakaya.
