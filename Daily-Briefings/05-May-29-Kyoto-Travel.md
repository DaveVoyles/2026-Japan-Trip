#  Day 5: Friday, May 29 (Travel: Tokyo -> Kyoto)

**Goal:** Bullet train, Gion, and Pontocho Alley.

##  Timeline (JST)

- **09:00 AM:** Limousine Bus -> Tokyo Station (Check with hotel if available, OR Taxi).
  - _Alt:_ Train (Shinjuku -> Tokyo Station).
- **10:00 AM:** **Shinkansen (Nozomi)**.
  - _Seat:_ Car X, Seat E (Mt Fuji View, Left Side).
  - _Food:_ Buy **Ekiben** (Bento) at Tokyo Station BEFORE gates.
- **12:15 PM:** Arrive Kyoto Station.
- **01:00 PM:** Taxi to **Cross Hotel Kyoto**.
  - _Address Card:_ [Show Japanese Address](../Reference/quick-reference-cards.md).
- **02:00 PM:** Explore **Nishiki Market** (400 year old market).
  - _Eat:_ Soy milk donuts, Octopus on a stick.
- **05:00 PM:** **Gion District (Geisha Spotting)**.
  - _Note:_ Stay on main streets. Private alleys are "No Photo" zones ($100 fine).
- **07:00 PM:** Dinner: **Pontocho Alley** (Riverside dining).
