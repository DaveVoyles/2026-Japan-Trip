# Hotel & Accommodation Options

## Constraints Checklist
- [x] **Budget:** Under  USD/night.
- [x] **Bed Style:** Western Beds ONLY (No Futons/Tatami).
- [x] **Location:** Near major transit hubs (Ginza, Kyoto Station, Namba).
