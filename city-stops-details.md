# City Stops & Sightseeing Details

## Tokyo

### Recommended things to see
- **Akihabara (Dave):**
  - *Super Potato:* 3 floors of retro games.
  - *Taito Station:* Claw machines and arcade games.
  - *BEEP:* Vintage PC games.
- **Shibuya/Parco (Dave):**
  - *Nintendo Tokyo:* Official merch store.
  - *Capcom Store:* Monster Hunter/Street Fighter goods.
- **Nakano Broadway (Dave):** Old school anime cels and rare toys.
- **Aoyama Flower Market Tea House (Wife):** Focus on the greenhouse ambience and flowers (Minato City).
- **Nezu Museum (Wife):** Stunning private garden (near Aoyama).
- **Kappabashi Street (Wife):** Buy realistic plastic food samples and Japanese ceramics.
- **Depachika (Basement Food Halls) (Wife):**
  - *Isetan Shinjuku:* The "king" of food halls. Incredible cakes/pastries.
  - *Ginza Mitsukoshi:* High-end fruits and sweets.
- **Stationery Shopping (Wife):**
  - *Itoya (Ginza):* 12 floors of stationery.
  - *Loft (Shibuya):* Massive variety of cute goods.

### Things to avoid
- **Roppongi Touts:** Do not follow anyone into a bar in Roppongi or Kabukicho who promises "cheap drinks."
- **Rush Hour Trains:** Avoid 7:30 AM - 9:30 AM on weekdays if you have luggage.
- **Eating while Walking:** It is generally frowned upon. Eat at the shop or find a designated spot.

### Things to know prior to this city
- **Getting Around:** The JR Yamanote Line (Green Loop) is your lifeline.
- **Navigation:** Google Maps is flawless. Follow the "Car Number" suggestions for easier transfers.
- **Escalators:** Stand on the **LEFT**, walk on the right.
- **Trash:** Public trash cans are rare. Carry a small plastic bag for your trash.

## Kyoto

### Recommended things to see
- **Fushimi Inari Taisha:** The "Fox" shrine. Hike up to Yotsutsuji intersection for views.
- **Kinkaku-ji:** The Golden Pavilion. Iconic but crowded.
- **Kiyomizu-dera:** Massive wooden stage with great views of the city.
- **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove:** Magical early in the morning.
- **Kyoto Botanical Gardens (Wife):** Great for autumn flowers (Cosmos, Roses).
- **Nishiki Market (Wife):** "Kyoto's Kitchen" - soy milk donuts, skewers, knives/spices.
- **Philosopher's Path:** Peaceful canal walk.

### Things to avoid
- **The Bus with Luggage:** Kyoto buses are notoriously crowded. Use the subway or taxis for luggage transport.
- **Gion Photography:** Respect the "No Photography" signs on private streets in Gion.
- **Late Starts:** Popular spots (Bamboo Grove, Fushimi Inari) are swamped by 9:00 AM. Go at 6:30 AM.

### Things to know prior to this city
- **Transport:** The train network is sparse compared to Tokyo. You will walk a lot.
- **Taxis:** Excellent for short hops between temples to save time/energy.
- **Vibe:** It is the cultural capital; the pace is slower, but the tourist density is higher in specific spots.

## Osaka

### Recommended things to see
- **Universal Studios Japan:** Super Nintendo World & Donkey Kong Country are the priorities.
- **Den Den Town (Dave):** Nipponbashi area. Great prices on retro games (often cheaper than Tokyo).
- **Dotonbori:** The neon heart of Osaka. See the Glico Man sign.
- **Food (Non-Sushi):**
  - *Rikuro Ojisan:* Famous jiggly cheesecake (Namba area).
  - *Okonomiyaki:* Mizuno (Dotonbori) is famous.
  - *Kushikatsu Daruma:* Fried skewers.

### Things to avoid
- **Touts in Dotonbori:** Ignore the guys trying to get you into girls' bars or overpriced izakayas.
- **Missing the Last Train:** Taxis are expensive if you stay out past midnight.

### Things to know prior to this city
- **Escalators:** Stand on the **RIGHT**, walk on the left (Opposite of Tokyo!).
- **Vibe:** People are louder, friendlier, and more direct than in Tokyo.
- **Kuidaore:** The motto "Eat until you drop" applies here. Street food is king.

## Nara (Day Trip)

### Recommended things to see
- **Nara Park:** Free roaming deer.
- **Todai-ji Temple:** Houses the Great Buddha (Daibutsu). Massive wooden structure.
- **Nakatanidou:** Famous mochi pounding shop (watch the speed pounding).

### Things to avoid
- **Aggressive Deer:** If you have "shika senbei" (deer crackers), they will swarm you. Hide the food if you want peace.
- **Deer Poop:** Watch your step in the park.

### Things to know prior to this city
- **Access:** Easy 45-minute train ride from Kyoto (Kintetsu Line is usually faster/closer to the park than JR).
- **Walking:** It is a walking city, mostly centered around the park area.
