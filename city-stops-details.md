# City Stops & Sightseeing Details

## Tokyo
### For Dave (Gaming/Tech)
- **Akihabara:**
    - *Super Potato:* 3 floors of retro games.
    - *Taito Station:* Claw machines and arcade games.
    - *BEEP:* Vintage PC games.
- **Shibuya/Parco:**
    - *Nintendo Tokyo:* Official merch store.
    - *Capcom Store:* Monster Hunter/Street Fighter goods.
- **Nakano Broadway:** (Optional) Old school anime cels and rare toys.

### For Wife (Flowers/Bakery/Shopping)
- **Aoyama Flower Market Tea House:** (Minato City) Focus on the greenhouse ambience and flowers.
- **Nezu Museum:** Stunning private garden (near Aoyama).
- **Kappabashi Street:** Buy realistic plastic food samples and Japanese ceramics.
- **Depachika (Basement Food Halls):**
    - *Isetan Shinjuku:* The "king" of food halls. Incredible cakes/pastries.
    - *Ginza Mitsukoshi:* High-end fruits and sweets.
- **Stationery Shopping:**
    - *Itoya (Ginza):* 12 floors of stationery.
    - *Loft (Shibuya):* Massive variety of cute goods.

## Kyoto
### Temples & Culture
- **Fushimi Inari:** The "Fox" shrine. Hike as far as you want.
- **Kinkaku-ji:** Golden Pavilion.
- **Kiyomizu-dera:** Massive wooden stage, great views.

### Gardens & Shopping
- **Kyoto Botanical Gardens:** Great for autumn flowers (Cosmos, Roses).
- **Nishiki Market:** "Kyoto's Kitchen" - try soy milk donuts, skewers, and shop for knives/spices.
- **Philosopher's Path:** Peaceful canal walk (good cafes nearby).

## Osaka
### Entertainment
- **Universal Studios Japan:** Super Nintendo World is the highlight.
- **Den Den Town:** Nipponbashi area. Great prices on retro games (often cheaper than Tokyo).

### Food (Non-Sushi)
- **Rikuro Ojisan:** Famous jiggly cheesecake (Namba area).
- **Okonomiyaki:** Mizuno (Dotonbori) is famous.
- **Kushikatsu Daruma:** Fried skewers (fun atmosphere).

## The "Real Fox" Option (Zao Fox Village)
*Note: This requires a full day trip from Tokyo.*
- **Location:** Shiroishi-Zao (Miyagi Prefecture).
- **Travel:** ~2 hrs Shinkansen + 20 min taxi.
- **Experience:** Open area with 100+ foxes roaming free.
- **Verdict:** Only do this if you extend the trip to 9-10 days.
