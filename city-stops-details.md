### Things to avoid
- **Roppongi Touts:** Do not follow anyone into a bar in Roppongi or Kabukicho who promises "cheap drinks."
- **Rush Hour Trains:** Avoid 7:30 AM - 9:30 AM on weekdays if you have luggage.
- **Eating while Walking:** It is generally frowned upon. Eat at the shop or find a designated spot.

### Things to know prior to this city
- **Getting Around:** The JR Yamanote Line (Green Loop) is your lifeline.
- **Navigation:** Google Maps is flawless. Follow the "Car Number" suggestions for easier transfers.
- **Escalators:** Stand on the **LEFT**, walk on the right.
- **Trash:** Public trash cans are rare. Carry a small plastic bag for your trash.

## Kyoto

### Recommended things to see

#### Temples & Shrines
| Est Cost | URL | Details | Availability | English Friendly |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Free | [Fushimi Inari Taisha](http://inari.jp/en/) | The "Fox" shrine with thousands of Torii gates; hike to Yotsutsuji for views. The further up you hike, the fewer tourists you will see. There are small tea houses along the way selling boiled eggs and kitsune udon. | Open 24/7 (Go at 6:30 AM) | High |
| $ | [Kinkaku-ji](https://www.shokoku-ji.jp/en/kinkakuji/) | The Golden Pavilion is iconic and photogenic, but very crowded. The reflection of the gold leaf in the pond is the money shot. It's a Zen temple, so despite the crowds, the grounds are manicured to perfection. | Walk-in | High |
| $ | [Kiyomizu-dera](https://www.kiyomizudera.or.jp/en/) | Massive wooden stage with great views; the walk up (Ninenzaka) is charming. The stage was built without using a single nail. Drink from the Otowa waterfall below for health, longevity, or success (pick one!). | Walk-in | High |

#### Nature & Culture
| Est Cost | URL | Details | Availability | English Friendly |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Free | [Arashiyama Bamboo Grove](https://kyoto.travel/en/sightseeing_information/sagano_arashiyama.html) | Towering bamboo stalks that are magical early morning, but a tourist trap by 10 AM. | $ | [Kyoto Botanical Gardens](https://www.kyotobotanicalgardens.jp/en/) | Great for autumn flowers (Cosmos, Roses) and less crowded than temples. It's the oldest botanical garden in Japan. A great place to take a break from the "temple fatigue" and just enjoy a quiet picnic. | Walk-in | Medium |
| Free | [Philosopher's Path](https://kyoto.travel/en/sightseeing_information/philosophers_path.html) | Peaceful canal walk lined with cherry trees (green in Oct) with good cafes nearby. It's named after a philosopher who used to meditate while walking here. The path connects the Silver Pavilion (Ginkaku-ji) to Nanzen-ji, making it a great transit route. | Open 24/7 | High |

#### Shopping & Food
| Est Cost | URL | Details | Availability | English Friendly |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $-$$ | [Nishiki Market](https://www.kyoto-nishiki.or.jp/) | "Kyoto's Kitchen" is a narrow street with soy milk donuts, skewers, and knives. It's a sensory overload of smells and sights. Try the "tako tamago" (a baby octopus with a quail egg inside its head) if you are adventurous. | Walk-in (10 AM - 5 PM) | Medium |

### Things to avoid
- **The Bus with Luggage:** Kyoto buses are notoriously crowded. Use the subway or taxis for luggage transport.
- **Gion Photography:** Respect the "No Photography" signs on private streets in Gion.
- **Late Starts:** Popular spots (Bamboo Grove, Fushimi Inari) are swamped by 9:00 AM. Go at 6:30 AM.

### Things to know prior to this city
- **Transport:** The train network is sparse compared to Tokyo. You will walk a lot.
- **Taxis:** Excellent for short hops between temples to save time/energy.
- **Vibe:** It is the cultural capital; the pace is slower, but the tourist density is higher in specific spots.

#### Food (Non-Sushi)
| Est Cost | URL | Details | Availability | English Friendly |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $ | [Rikuro Ojisan](http://www.rikuro.co.jp/en/) | Famous jiggly cheesecake that is fluffy and light, located in Namba. They ring a bell every time a fresh batch comes out of the oven. The cheesecake is surprisingly eggy and not too sweet, more like a soufflé. | Walk-in (Expect line) | Medium |

### Things to avoid
- **Touts in Dotonbori:** Ignore the guys trying to get you into girls' bars or overpriced izakayas.
- **Missing the Last Train:** Taxis are expensive if you stay out past midnight.

### Things to know prior to this city
- **Escalators:** Stand on the **RIGHT**, walk on the left (Opposite of Tokyo!).
- **Vibe:** People are louder, friendlier, and more direct than in Tokyo.
- **Kuidaore:** The motto "Eat until you drop" applies here. Street food is king.

## Nara (Day Trip)

### Recommended things to see
| Est Cost | URL | Details | Availability | English Friendly |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Free | [Nara Park](https://www.visitnara.jp/venues/A00659/) | Free roaming deer where you can buy crackers to feed them. The deer are considered messengers of the gods. They have learned to bow to tourists to ask for food, which is both cute and slightly aggressive. | Open 24/7 | High |
| $ | [Todai-ji Temple](https://www.todaiji.or.jp/en/) | Houses the Great Buddha (Daibutsu) in a massive wooden structure. It was once the largest wooden building in the world. Look for the pillar with a hole in the base; squeezing through it is said to bring enlightenment. | Walk-in | High |
| $ | [Nakatanidou](http://www.nakatanidou.jp/) | Famous mochi pounding shop where you can watch the high-speed pounding show. The pounding happens every 30 minutes or so and draws a huge crowd. The resulting mochi (yomogi flavor with red bean) is incredibly soft and warm. | Walk-in | Medium |

### Things to avoid
- **Aggressive Deer:** If you have "shika senbei" (deer crackers), they will swarm you. Hide the food if you want peace.
- **Deer Poop:** Watch your step in the park.

### Things to know prior to this city
- **Access:** Easy 45-minute train ride from Kyoto (Kintetsu Line is usually faster/closer to the park than JR).
- **Walking:** It is a walking city, mostly centered around the park area.
