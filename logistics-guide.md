# Japan Trip Logistics Guide for Dave & Debbie

##  Connectivity: eSIM Setup (iPhone 15)

Since you both have iPhone 15s (which are eSIM-only in the US), you will need to set up an eSIM data plan. This is much easier than a physical SIM or Pocket Wi-Fi.

### Recommended Providers
We recommend **Ubigi** or **Airalo**. Both use the Docomo or Softbank networks (excellent coverage).

| Feature | [Ubigi](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ubigi-esim-internet-data/id1435567041) | [Airalo (Moshi Moshi)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/airalo-esim-phone-internet/id1475911720) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost (Approx)** | ~ USD for 10GB | ~ USD for 10GB |
| **Validity** | 30 Days | 30 Days |
| **Network** | Docomo (Top Tier) | Softbank / KDDI |
| **Pros** | Often faster; easy app setup. | Very popular; user-friendly app. |
| **Guides** | [Official Install Guide](https://cellulardata.ubigi.com/help-center/install-esim-iphone/) | [Official Install Guide](https://www.airalo.com/help/getting-started/how-to-install-and-activate-an-esim-on-your-ios-device) |

### Step-by-Step Setup (Do this 1-2 days before flying)

1.  **Download the App:**
    *   Download **Ubigi** or **Airalo** from the App Store while still in the US.
2.  **Create an Account:**
    *   Sign up using your email.
3.  **Purchase a Plan:**
    *   Select "Japan".
    *   Choose a data amount (10GB is usually plenty for 2 weeks if using hotel Wi-Fi).
    *   Pay with Apple Pay or Credit Card.
4.  **Install the eSIM:**
    *   Follow the prompts to "Install eSIM".
    *   **Label it:** Name it "Japan Travel" or "Ubigi" so you don't confuse it with your primary US line.
    *   **Turn it OFF:** Ensure the line is toggled **OFF** until you land in Japan.

### Upon Arrival in Japan

1.  **Turn ON the eSIM:** Go to Settings > Cellular and toggle your "Japan Travel" line **ON**.
2.  **Data Roaming:** Turn **Data Roaming ON** for the *Japan* line.
3.  **Primary Line:** Keep your US line ON if you want to receive iMessages/Calls (check your carrier's roaming fees!), or turn it OFF to avoid charges.
    *   *Tip:* Set "Cellular Data" to use the Japan eSIM.

## Transit: Suica / PASMO (Apple Wallet)

You do **not** need physical train cards. You can add them directly to your iPhones.

### How to Add (Do this anytime)

- **Guide:** [Apple Support: Add Suica/PASMO to Apple Wallet](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT207155)

1. Open **Apple Wallet**.
2. Tap the **(+)** button in the top right.
3. Select **Transit Card**.
4. Search for **"Suica"** or **"PASMO"** (ICOCA is also fine).
5. **Add Money:** Load 1,000 - 2,000 (~$7-14 USD) to start.
   - *Note:* **Mastercard** and **Amex** work best. Visa sometimes has issues reloading foreign transit cards in Apple Wallet.

### How to Use

- **Trains/Subways:** Tap the top of your iPhone on the yellow reader at the gate. You do **not** need to wake the phone or use FaceID (if "Express Transit" mode is on).
- **Vending Machines / Konbini:** Look for the Suica/Pasmo logo and tap to pay.

## Money & Payments

- **7-Eleven ATMs:** The best place to withdraw cash. They accept US debit cards and have English menus.
- **Coin Purse:** You will get a LOT of coins. Bring a small coin purse or buy a cute one there.
- **Credit Cards:** Accepted at most hotels, department stores, and large restaurants.
- **Cash:** Still king for small shrines, street food, and some ticket machines.
