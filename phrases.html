🗣️ Japanese Phrases — Quick Reference

Survival phrases for Tokyo · Kyoto · Osaka · trains · restaurants · shopping

💡 Pronunciation tip: Japanese vowels are consistent: A = "ah", I = "ee", U = "oo", E = "eh", O = "oh". Stress is mostly flat — don't over-emphasize any syllable.

🙏 Essential Basics

JapaneseRomajiMeaning / When to Use
ありがとうございますArigatou gozaimasuThank you (polite)
Use this everywhere — people appreciate it
すみませんSumimasenExcuse me / Sorry
Works as "excuse me to get attention" AND "I'm sorry" — most useful word in Japan
はいHaiYes
いいえIieNo (rarely needed — Japanese rarely say this directly)
わかりませんWakarimasenI don't understand
Say this clearly and most staff will switch to gestures or a phone
英語が話せますか？Eigo ga hanasemasu ka?Do you speak English?
もう一度お願いしますMou ichido onegaishimasuPlease say that again
これをくださいKore wo kudasaiI'll have this / Please give me this
Point at the item — works in any shop or restaurant
〜はどこですか？[place] wa doko desu ka?Where is [place]?
e.g. "Toire wa doko desu ka?" = Where is the toilet?

👋 Greetings & Social

JapaneseRomajiMeaning
おはようございますOhayou gozaimasuGood morning (until ~10 AM)
こんにちはKonnichiwaGood afternoon / Hello (10 AM – 6 PM)
こんばんはKonbanwaGood evening (after 6 PM)

Say it every time before a meal; locals will smile

Say this when leaving a restaurant or finishing a meal

Used at first meetings and when asking for a favor

🍜 Restaurant & Food

🍣 Pointing at pictures or a plastic food display always works. Many restaurants have picture menus — ask "shashin ga arimasu ka?" (do you have a photo menu?).
JapaneseRomajiMeaning
〜人です[number]-nin desuWe are [number] people
e.g. "futari desu" = two people
予約しましたYoyaku shimashitaI have a reservation
席はありますか？Seki wa arimasu ka?Do you have a table?
おすすめは何ですか？Osusume wa nan desu ka?What do you recommend?
これはなんですか？Kore wa nan desu ka?What is this?
〜アレルギーがあります[food] arerugii ga arimasuI'm allergic to [food]
辛くないものKarakunai monoSomething not spicy
お水くださいOmizu kudasaiWater please
Water is usually free and will be brought automatically
お会計お願いしますOkaikei onegaishimasuCheck / Bill please
Usually you pay at the register, not the table
カードで払えますか？Kaado de haraemasu ka?Can I pay by card?
おいしい！Oishii!Delicious!
Say this and the kitchen staff will love you

🛍️ Shopping

JapaneseRomajiMeaning
いくらですか？Ikura desu ka?How much is this?
高すぎますTakasugi masuThat's too expensive
Bargaining is not common in Japan — use politely only at markets
これをくださいKore wo kudasaiI'll take this / Give me this
袋は要りませんFukuro wa irimasenNo bag needed
Japan now charges for plastic bags; say this if you have a tote
税抜き価格ですか？Zeinuki kakaku desu ka?Is this the pre-tax price?
10% consumption tax applies; posted prices are sometimes pre-tax
免税できますか？Menzei dekimasu ka?Can I get tax-free (duty-free)?
Passport required; minimum spend ~¥5,500 at most stores
試着できますか？Shichaku dekimasu ka?Can I try this on?

🏨 Hotel

JapaneseRomajiMeaning

チェックアウトお願いしますChekkauto onegaishimasuCheck-out please
荷物を預けられますか？Nimotsu wo azukeraremasu ka?Can I store my luggage?
Most hotels hold bags before check-in and after check-out

🆘 Emergencies

📞 Japan emergency numbers: Police: 110 · Fire/Ambulance: 119. Both have English operator options.
JapaneseRomajiMeaning
助けてください！Tasukete kudasai!Help me please!
警察を呼んでくださいKeisatsu wo yonde kudasaiPlease call the police
救急車を呼んでくださいKyuukyuusha wo yonde kudasaiPlease call an ambulance
病院はどこですか？Byouin wa doko desu ka?Where is the hospital?
具合が悪いですGuai ga warui desuI feel sick / I'm not well
財布を盗まれましたSaifu wo nusumaremashitaMy wallet was stolen
パスポートをなくしましたPasupooto wo nakushimashitaI lost my passport
